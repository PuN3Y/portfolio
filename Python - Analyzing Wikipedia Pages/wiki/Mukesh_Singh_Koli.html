Mukesh Singh Koli is an Indian politician, social worker and former Member of Legislative Assembly for Pauri Assembly constituency as a member of Bharatiya Janata Party.[1] In 2017 Uttarakhand Legislative Assembly election, Koli defeated the Naval Kishor of Indian National Congress party by the margin of 7,030 votes.[2] Mukesh Singh Koli is serving as State-Incharge of Bharatiya Janata Party Minority Front for Uttrakhand.

Early life

[edit]

Mukesh Singh Koli was born to the Dalip Singh Koli in Prachiyali village of Pauri Garhwal district of Uttrakhand.[3]
