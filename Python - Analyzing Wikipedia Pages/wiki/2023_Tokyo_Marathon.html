The 2023 Tokyo Marathon was the 16th edition of the annual marathon race in Tokyo, held on Sunday, 5 March 2023.[1][2][3] A Platinum Label marathon, it was the first of six annual World Marathon Majors events to be held in 2023, and the only one of the six that did not announce the inclusion of a non-binary division for the year.[4][5] The field for the marathon was limited to 37,500 runners.[1]
