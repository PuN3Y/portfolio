Fiona Brown (born 1985)[1] is a World and European champion bridge player.

Bridge accomplishments

[edit]

Wins

[edit]
  • European Women Teams Championship (2), 2012,[2] 2016[3]
  • World Bridge Games (2), 2012,[4] 2018 [5]

Runners-up

[edit]
  • Venice Cup (2), 2013,[6] 2017[7]
  • European Women Teams Championship (1), 2014[8]
