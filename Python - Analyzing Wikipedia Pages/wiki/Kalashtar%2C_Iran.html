Kalashtar (Persian: كلشتر)[a] is a suburb of Rudbar city,[4] and a village in Kalashtar Rural District[5] of the Central District in Rudbar County, Gilan province, Iran. It was the former seat of the rural district. The capital was transferred to the village of Jamalabad-e Kuseh in 1994,[6] and again transferred to the village of Aliabad in 2024.[7]

Environment

[edit]

Kalashtar is 3 km (1.9 mi) away from the city center of Rudbar, at the foot of the Western Alborz Mountains, at an altitude of 550 m (1,800 ft) above sea level, overlooking the Rudbar. Due to the Mediterranean climate, olive groves have developed well in Kalashtar, making olive the main product of the village.[8]

A waterfall, more than high is one of the village's tourist attractions. Water Springs such as Cheshmeh Bar (Gilaki: Chimeh Var (چیمه وار)), the dense and pristine forests as well as Aseman Sara Mountain and plains with tall, green grass at an altitude of 1,900 m (6,200 ft) above sea level are other attractions of Kolshtar.[8]

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

Klashtar was formerly in Rahmatabad Rural District of the county. According to the 1976 census, it had both tap water and power connection. It also had elementary and middle school.[9]

According to the 1986 census, Kaleshtar also had medical clinic, and access to public transportation.[10]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 1,272 in 409 households.[11] The following census in 2011 counted 1,314 people in 438 households.[12]

The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 32 people in 11 households,[2] as most of the village was incorporated to Rudbar's urban area.
