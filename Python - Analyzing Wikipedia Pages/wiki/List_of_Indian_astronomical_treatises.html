Indian Astronomical Treatises
This article is an orphan, as no other articles link to it. Please introduce links to this page from related articles. (April 2022)

Ancient India was one of the most important seat of Astronomical studies. There were many scholars, philosophers and astronomers in ancient India, who wrote treatises on experimental and mathematical astronomy. Most of the Ancient Indian Astronomical Treatises were written and composed in Sanskrit language.[1][2][3][4][5]

List of the Astronomical Treatises

[edit]
  1. Vedanga Jyotisha
  2. Aryabhatiya
  3. Brahmasphuta-siddhanta
  4. Pañcasiddhāntikā
  5. Mahabhaskariya
  6. Laghubhaskariya
  7. Aryabhatiyabhashya
  8. Śisyadhīvrddhida
  9. Siddhāntatilaka
  10. Siddhāntaśiromani
  11. Karanakutūhala
  12. Siddhāntaśekhara
  13. Yantra-rāja
  14. Jyotirmimamsa
  15. Sphutanirnaya
  16. Karanottama
  17. Uparāgakriyākrama
  18. Śiṣyadhīvṛddhidatantra
  19. Brihat-Samhita
  20. Grahana-Maala
  21. Lilavati
  22. Shatapatha Brahmana
  23. Surya Siddhanta
  24. Makarandasarini
  25. Mahadevi (astronomy book)
  26. Rājamṛgāṅka (astronomy book)
  27. Jagadbhūṣaṇa
  28. Grahacaranibandhana and lost text Mahamarganibandhana
  29. Tantrasamgraha
  30. Karanapaddhati
  31. Venvaroha
