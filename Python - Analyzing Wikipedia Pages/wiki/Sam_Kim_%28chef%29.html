Kim Hee-Tae (Korean: 김희태; born May 8, 1977),[citation needed] better known by his English-language name Sam Kim (샘 킴) is a South Korean chef and television personality.[1][unreliable source?] He was a cast member in the variety show Real Men and Please Take Care of My Refrigerator.[2] He also hosted Sam and Raymon Cooking Time from 2011 to 2012.
