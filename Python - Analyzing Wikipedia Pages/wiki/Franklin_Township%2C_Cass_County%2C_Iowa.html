Franklin Township is one of sixteen townships in Cass County, Iowa, United States. As of the 2000 census, its population was 397.

Geography

[edit]

Franklin Township covers an area of 34.64 square miles (89.7 km2) and contains one incorporated settlement, Wiota. According to the USGS, it contains three cemeteries: Franklin Lutheran, Norway Center and Wiota.
