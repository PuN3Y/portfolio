Quinprenaline (INNTooltip International Nonproprietary Name; also known as quinterenol) is a sympathomimetic, long-acting β-adrenergic receptor agonist, and bronchodilator of the phenethylamine family that was never approved for human use.[1][2][3][4][5] It was first described by the 1960s.[2][4]
