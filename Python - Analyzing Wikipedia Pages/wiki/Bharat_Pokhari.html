Bharat Pokhari is a town in Kaski District in the Gandaki Province of northern-central Nepal. At the 1991 Nepal census, it had a population of 8,889 persons living in 1782 individual households. Bharat Pokhari was a Village Development Committee, and it was merged with Lekhnath Municipality.[1]

The elevation of Bharat Pokhari ranges from 657m to 1600m.
