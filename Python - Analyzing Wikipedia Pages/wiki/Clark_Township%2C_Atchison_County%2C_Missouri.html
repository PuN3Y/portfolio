Clark Township is a township in Atchison County, Missouri, United States.[2] At the 2020 census, its population was 898.[3]

History

[edit]

Clark Township was established in 1845[4] and was one of the five original townships in Atchison County.[5] it was reduced to its present limits at the establishment of Dale Township in 1876.[6]

A ferry in the southwest of the township crossed the Missouri River to St. Deroin in Nebraska.

Geography

[edit]

Clark Township covers an area of 75.37 square miles (195.2 km2) and contains one incorporated settlement, Fairfax. Two other former settlements were present: Milton and Nishnabotna

The streams of Cow Branch, Old Channel Nishnabotna River, and Rock Creek run through this township. It contains five cemeteries: Beck, English Grove, Milton, Mount Hope, and Pleasant Ridge.[7]

Deroin Bend Island, an island of the Missouri River, is partly located in the southwesternmost portion of the township but is mostly located in Lincoln Township, Holt County.[8]

Transportation

[edit]

Clark Township contains one airport, Steele Airport.
