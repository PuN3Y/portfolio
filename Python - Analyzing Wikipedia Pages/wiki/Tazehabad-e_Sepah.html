Tazehabad-e Sepah (Persian: تازه ابادسپاه)[a] is a village in Goharbaran-e Shomali Rural District[4] of Goharbaran District in Miandorud County, Mazandaran province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 217 in 55 households, when it was in Rudpey-ye Shomali Rural District[b] of the Central District in Sari County.[6] The following census in 2011 counted 217 people in 66 households,[7] by which time the rural district had been separated from the district in the formation of Rudpey-ye Shomali District and renamed Farahabad-e Shomali Rural District.[5] Tazehabad-e Sepah was separated from the county in the establishment of Miandorud County and transferred to Goharbaran-e Shomali Rural District created in the new Goharbaran District.[4] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 271 people in 92 households.[2]
