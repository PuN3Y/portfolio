Mubarikpur is a village in the Bhopal district of Madhya Pradesh, India. It is located in the Huzur tehsil and the Phanda block.[1] Hindi is the local language.

Demographics

[edit]

According to the 2011 census of India, Mubarikpur has 248 households. The effective literacy rate (i.e. the literacy rate of population excluding children aged 6 and below) is 69.43%.[2]
