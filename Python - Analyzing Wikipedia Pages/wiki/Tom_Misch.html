Musical artist

Thomas Abraham Misch (born 25 June 1995) is an English musician and producer. He began releasing music on SoundCloud in 2012[7] and released his debut studio album Geography in 2018.[8] In 2020, he released his second studio album What Kinda Music in collaboration with English jazz drummer Yussef Dayes,[9] distributed through Blue Note Records and Caroline.[10] The Album was nominated for the Best Album Ivor Novello Award in 2021. In 2023 he released his third studio album Happy Music, in "collaboration" with his alter-ego, Supershy.[11]

Early life and education

[edit]

Misch studied music technology at Langley Park School for Boys[12] and later, in 2014, enrolled in a jazz guitar course at Trinity Laban Conservatoire of Music and Dance in Greenwich but left after six months to focus on his own music.[3] He began learning to play the violin at age 4, later learning to play the guitar.[13]
