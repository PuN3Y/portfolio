Kaiwi Lyman-Mersereau (born May 13, 1983), or simple Kaiwi Lyman, is an American actor from Honolulu, Hawaii. He is known for his co-starring roles in Den of Thieves,[1][2] American Violence[3] and Hickok.[4][5]
