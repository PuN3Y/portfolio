Kalmar Airport (IATA: KLR, ICAO: ESMQ), branded as Kalmar Öland Airport, is an airport in south-eastern Sweden. The airport is located 5 kilometres west of downtown Kalmar. The airfield was originally the home of the Kalmar Wing (F 12). The airport is owned by Kalmar Municipality and Kalmar County.
