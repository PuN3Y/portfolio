Habiba Sadou (Arabic: حبيبة سعدو; born 1 November 1986) is an Algerian international footballer who plays as a defender for Afak Relizane and for the Algeria women's national football team. She competed for Algeria at the 2018 Africa Women Cup of Nations, playing in two matches.[1][2]
