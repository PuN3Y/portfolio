Topolobampo is a Mexican restaurant in Chicago, Illinois.[1][2][3] Chef and television personality Rick Bayless and Deann Bayless opened Topolobampo in 1989. It won the James Beard Foundation Award for Outstanding Restaurant in 2017.[4] Topolobampo holds one Michelin star.[5]
