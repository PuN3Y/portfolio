From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Indian businessman (died 2010)

Pravinchandra Varjivan Gandhi (?–2010) was chairman of Dena Bank and a leading newspaper publisher from India. He was awarded the Padma Bhushan in 2002.[1]
