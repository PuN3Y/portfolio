Stanley Berryhill III (born June 8, 1998) is an American professional football wide receiver for the BC Lions of the Canadian Football League (CFL). He played college football at Arizona.[3][4][5]

Atlanta Falcons

[edit]

On May 2, 2022, Berryhill III signed with the Atlanta Falcons as an undrafted free agent.[7] He was waived on August 30, 2022.[8]

Arizona Cardinals

[edit]

On September 20, 2022, Berryhill III signed with the practice squad of the Arizona Cardinals.[9] He was released off the practice squad on October 18, 2022.[10]

Detroit Lions

[edit]

On October 19, 2022, Berryhill III signed with the practice squad of the Detroit Lions.[11] He was elevated to the active roster on October 27, 2022, and then reverted to the practice squad after the game.[12] He was signed to the active roster on November 5, then waived and re-signed to the practice squad.[13] He signed a reserve/future contract on January 9, 2023.[14]

On April 21, 2023, Berryhill III was suspended six games for violating the league’s gambling policy after betting on college football games at the Lions' facility.[15] He was released on May 10, 2023.[16] After the league adjusted its gambling policy for players, Berryhill III's suspension was reduced to four games and was reinstated on October 3, 2023.[17]

Houston Roughnecks

[edit]

On December 12, 2023, Berryhill III signed with the Houston Roughnecks of the XFL.[18] The Roughnecks brand was transferred to the Houston Gamblers when the XFL and United States Football League merged to create the United Football League (UFL).[19]

BC Lions

[edit]

Berryhill III signed with the BC Lions of the Canadian Football League on January 16, 2024.[20] On June 2, 2024, Berryhill III was re-assigned to the Practice Squad.[21] He rejoined the active roster on June 20, 2024. On June 19, 2025, Berryhill III was placed on the Lions' 1-game injured list. He rejoined the active roster on July 4, 2025.[22] On June 18, 2026, Berryhill III was placed on the Lions' 6-game injured list. He rejoined the active roster on August 12, 2026.[23]
