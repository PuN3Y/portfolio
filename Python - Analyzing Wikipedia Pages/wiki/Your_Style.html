"Your Style" is the fifth single from J. Williams' debut album, Young Love. The song features Erakah. The official remix also features Tyree.,[2] and is on the Collector's Edition of Williams' album.[3][4][5]

Music video

[edit]

The music video uses the remixed track, featuring both Erakah and Tyree. It was released on 14 December 2009.
