Carlos António Dinis (born April 4, 1966 in Luanda) is an Angolan basketball head coach. He is a brother of Angolan former football player Joaquim Dinis.

Career

[edit]

Dinis is the head coach of Angolan side Atlético Sport Aviação (ASA), while also being the head coach of Angola's U18 basketball team.[1]

In February 2016, he was appointed head coach of the Angolan senior basketball team, ahead of the qualification tournament for the 2016 Olympic games.[2]
