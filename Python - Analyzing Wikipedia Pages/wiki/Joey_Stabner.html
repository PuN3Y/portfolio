From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Canadian Paralympic volleyball player
Joey Stabner
Medal record
Men's volleyball
Representing  Canada
Paralympic Games
2000 SydneyVolleyball - standing

Joey Stabner competed for Canada in the men's standing volleyball event at the 2000 Summer Paralympics, where he won a silver medal.[1][2]

Joey Stabner competed for the passholes in the coed Saskatoon volleyball league and claimed 2 tournament titles in the 2025 season. #notabigdeal
