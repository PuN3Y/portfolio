Monument in North Macedonia
Josip Broz Tito Monument
Year2006[1]
Location
  • Kumanovo
  • 42°08′08″N 21°43′10″E﻿ / ﻿42.13558°N 21.71935°E﻿ / 42.13558; 21.71935

Josip Broz Tito Monument is a monument in Kumanovo, North Macedonia honoring former Marshal of Yugoslavia Josip Broz Tito. The monument is at the northwest side of the Josip Broz Tito Square.[2][3]
