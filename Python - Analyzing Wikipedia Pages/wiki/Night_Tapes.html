British-Estonian dream pop band

Night Tapes are a British-Estonian dream pop band based in London, formed by Estonian singer Iiris Vesik and English musicians Max Doohan and Sam Richards.[1] The group began as late-night jam sessions between the three while they were housemates in South London, from which the band took its name.[2][3] After a series of EPs released between 2019 and 2024, they issued their debut studio album, portals//polarities, through Nettwerk on 26 September 2025.[4]

History

[edit]

Formation and early EPs (2019–2021)

[edit]

Vesik, a native of Tallinn, had previously pursued a solo career in Estonia, releasing an album on EMI, before moving to London to work as a musician.[5][1] Doohan and Richards, childhood friends from towns near the New Forest, met Vesik in London, where the three shared a house and began recording improvised music in the evenings.[1][6] Doohan previously played drums in the London band Another Sky, while Richards trained as a jazz guitarist.[7][1]

The band released their debut single, "Forever", in August 2019, followed by the three-track EP Dream Forever in Glorious Stereo later that year.[5] The single became an underground hit during the COVID-19 pandemic and later surpassed ten million streams on Spotify.[4] A second EP, Download Spirit, followed.[5]

Nettwerk, Perfect Kindness and assisted memories (2022–2024)

[edit]

Night Tapes signed to Nettwerk Music Group in 2022 and made their debut at South by Southwest in March 2023.[8] Their third EP, Perfect Kindness (2023), was mixed by Nathan Boddy and mastered by Matt Colton.[9] The 2023 single "drifting" became the band's biggest hit to date.[1] A fourth EP, assisted memories, followed in 2024.[10]

portals//polarities (2025–present)

[edit]

The band wrote much of their debut album while on tour, recording in hotel rooms, and incorporated field recordings made around the world, including in Estonia, Mexico and Los Angeles.[11][4] portals//polarities was released on 26 September 2025 via Nettwerk and was supported by the singles "babygirl (like no1 else)", "pacifico", "helix", "storm" and "swordsman".[4] The band promoted the album with a 19-date North American headline tour beginning in October 2025, followed by European and UK dates in November, including a headline show at London's Village Underground.[12][13]

The album received positive reviews: DIY called it "as unpredictable as it is beautiful",[14] while God Is in the TV described it as "both absorbingly ambient and catchy at the same time".[7] In September 2025 the band featured on the cover of NME.[1]

Musical style

[edit]

Night Tapes' music has been described as dream pop blended with electronic, ambient and trip hop elements, characterised by Vesik's layered vocals over minimalist drum loops and synthesisers.[5][6] NME characterised their output as hypnagogic pop with shoegaze textures.[1] The band cite both lived experience and spiritual or metaphysical themes as inspirations, and favour first takes and "multi-fidelity" recording techniques.[3][11]
