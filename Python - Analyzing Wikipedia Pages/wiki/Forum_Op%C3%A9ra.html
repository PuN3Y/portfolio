From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
For other uses, see Forum Opéra (disambiguation).
Swiss operatic association

Forum Opéra—also known as AAOL or Association des amis de l'opéra de Lausanne / Association of Friends of the Lausanne Opera House—is a Swiss operatic association that promotes opera and bel canto within the French-speaking part of Switzerland.

History

[edit]

Forum Opéra is based on Swiss law according to articles 60 et seq. of Swiss Civil Code of 10 December 1907.[1][2]

Created on 17 August 1987,[3] it is chaired by its founder, Georges Reymond, a Swiss lawyer. It is sponsored by Franco-Swiss mezzo-soprano Renée Auphan,[note 1] and by Swiss tenor Éric Tappy.[4]

Among additional goals,[5] the organization aims to support and promote opera in Romandie and, above all, in Lausanne.[4]

Notes

[edit]
  1. ↑ Renée Auphan was the former director of Théâtre municipal de Lausanne before taking over as director of Grand Théâtre de Genève.
