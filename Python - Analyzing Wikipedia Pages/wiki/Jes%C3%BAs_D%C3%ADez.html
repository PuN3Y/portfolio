Jesús Díez Pinillos (31 May 1931 – 28 March 2003) was a Spanish epee, foil and sabre fencer. He competed in four events at the 1960 Summer Olympics.[2] He competed at the 1959 Mediterranean Games where he won a bronze medal in the team épée event.[3]
