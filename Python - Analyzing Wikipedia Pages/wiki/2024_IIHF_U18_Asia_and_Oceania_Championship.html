The 2024 IIHF U18 Asia and Oceania Championship was an international men's under-18 ice hockey tournament organized by the International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF). The tournament took place from 23 to 30 April 2024 in Tashkent and Samarkand, Uzbekistan. It was the third under-18 edition of the event.[1][2][3][4][5]
