Line 2 of the Nantong Rail Transit (Chinese: 南通轨道交2号线; pinyin: Nántōng Guǐdào Jiāotōng èr hào xiàn) is a rapid transit line in Nantong, Jiangsu, China. It runs from Xingfu station to Xianfeng station.[1] The line is 20.85 kilometres (13.0 mi) in length with 17 underground stations, of which one station Nantong Railway Station is suspended for operation due to lack of enough entrances.[2]

History

[edit]

Construction began on October 26, 2018.[3] Test operation started on June 19, 2023[4] and trial-ride activity started 8–10 December 2023.[5] The line started operation on December 27, 2023.[6][7]
