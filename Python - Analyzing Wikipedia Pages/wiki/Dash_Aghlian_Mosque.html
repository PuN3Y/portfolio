The Dash Aghlian Mosque (Persian: مسجد داش آغلیان; Arabic: مسجد داش آغليان) is a Shi'ite mosque located on Shahid Samadzadeh Street, in Khoy, in the province of West Azerbaijan, Iran. The mosque was completed in the 19th century, during the Qajar era.[1][2][3]

The mosque was added to the Iran National Heritage List on 24 August 1999, administered by the Cultural Heritage, Handicrafts and Tourism Organization of Iran.
