Darrell Lanigan (born June 3, 1970) is an American professional stock car racing driver who has competed in the NASCAR Busch Series and the ARCA Re/Max Series.

Lanigan has also previously competed in series such as the World of Outlaws, the Lucas Oil Late Model Dirt Series, the Stacker 2 Xtreme DirtCar Series, the Northern Xtreme DirtCar Series, and the DIRTcar Nationals.[1]
