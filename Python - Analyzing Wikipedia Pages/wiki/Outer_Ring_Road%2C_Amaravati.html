The Outer Ring Road is a proposed 189.9 kilometers[1] road in the Andhra Pradesh Capital Region Development Authority (APCRDA) spread over Guntur and Krishna districts.[2] Guntur-Tenali-Vuyyuru-Amaravati urban alignment. It is being built by the National Highways Authority of India under Phase–VII of National Highways Development Project.[3]
