From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Urban administrative region in Minas Gerais, Brazil
See also: List of Intermediate and Immediate Geographic Regions of Minas Gerais

The Immediate Geographic Region of Além Paraíba is one of the 10 immediate geographic regions in the Intermediate Geographic Region of Juiz de Fora, one of the 70 immediate geographic regions in the Brazilian state of Minas Gerais and one of the 509 of Brazil, created by the National Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) in 2017.[1]

Municipalities

[edit]

It comprises 5 municipalities.

  • Além Paraíba   
  • Estrela Dalva     
  • Pirapetinga     
  • Santo Antônio do Aventureiro     
  • Volta Grande
