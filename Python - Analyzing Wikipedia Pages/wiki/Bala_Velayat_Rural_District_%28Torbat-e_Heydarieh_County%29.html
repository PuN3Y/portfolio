Bala Velayat Rural District (Persian: دهستان بالاولايت) is in the Central District of Torbat-e Heydarieh County, Razavi Khorasan province, Iran.[5]

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the rural district's population was 20,908 in 5,698 households.[6] There were 24,381 inhabitants in 6,916 households at the following census of 2011.[7] The 2016 census measured the population of the rural district as 27,133 in 7,978 households. The most populous of its 41 villages was Manzar, with 3,111 people.[4]
