The 2026 Pan-American Men’s Lacrosse Championship is an upcoming international men's lacrosse competition held in Kingston, Jamaica from August 17 to 23, 2026. It is hosted by the Jamaica Lacrosse Association with support of the Jamaica Olympic Association.[1][2]

It is the inaugural edition of the Pan-American Men’s Lacrosse Championship, which is sanctioned under the Pan-American Lacrosse Association (PALA) and will determine the five teams which will qualify for the 2027 World Lacrosse Men's Championship in Tokyo, Japan.[3] The rest of the teams will be eligible to play in Division II.[4]
