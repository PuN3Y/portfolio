Fernando José Pacheco Rivas (born 26 June 1999) is a Peruvian professional footballer who plays for Israeli club Hapoel Ironi Kiryat Shmona, as a winger.

Club career

[edit]

Sporting Cristal

[edit]

Born in Bujama Baja, Mala, Cañete, Pacheco joined Sporting Cristal's youth setup at the age of 14.[1] He made his first team debut on 20 August 2016 at the age of just 17, starting in a 1–0 home win against Unión Comercio.[2]

Pacheco scored his first professional goal on 3 March 2018, in a 5–0 routing of Universidad San Martín de Porres.[3]

Fluminense

[edit]

On 13 January 2020, Pacheco moved abroad and signed a four-year contract with Campeonato Brasileiro Série A side Fluminense.[4]

Emmen

[edit]

On 4 July 2022, Pacheco joined Emmen in the Netherlands on a season-long loan.[5]
