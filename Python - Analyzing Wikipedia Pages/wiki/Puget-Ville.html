Puget, officially Puget-Ville (French pronunciation: [pyʒɛ vil]; Occitan: Puget Vila) is a town and commune in the Var department in the Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur region in southeastern France.

Administration

[edit]

Max Bastide was elected mayor in 2001 and re-elected in 2008. In 2014, Catherine Altare was elected mayor. She was re-elected in 2020.[1]
