Muan–Gwangju Expressway (Korean: 무안광주고속도로) also known as 12th Expressway is an expressway in South Korea connecting Muan County to Gwangju.[2]

Formerly, this route designated as part of the Olympic Expressway but on January 3, 2008, it was separated into two segments that shared the same designation number with Gwangju-Daegu Expressway.

History

[edit]
  • November 8, 2007: Muan Airport IC - Naju IC (30.4 km) opened.[3]
  • May 28, 2008: Naju IC - Unsu IC (11 km) opened.[4]
  • September 29, 2009: West Gwangsan IC opened.[5]
