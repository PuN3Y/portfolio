Kagalnitskaya (Russian: Кагальницкая) is a rural locality (a stanitsa) and the administrative center of Kagalnitsky District of Rostov Oblast, Russia. Population: 6,831 (2010 census);[1] 6,848 (2002 census);[4] 5,983 (1989 Soviet census).[5]
