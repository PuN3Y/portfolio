Seo Young (Korean: 서영; born June 21, 1984) is a South Korean actress. She debuted in the 2006 Munhwa Broadcasting Corporation (MBC) drama What's Up Fox. In 2010, she started her own clothing store called Shine S.[2][3][4][5]

Career

[edit]

Debut: 2006–2007

[edit]

Starring in the OCN four-part series Temptation of Eve, she appeared in Her Own Art (or Technique) which was the fourth part.[6][7][8][9]

2008–2010

[edit]

In a spin-off of 1 Night 2 Days, she appeared alongside Kim Sook, Yoo Chae-yeong, Kim Yi-ji and Mi-ra in 1 Night 2 Days of Beauty.[10][11] A Tale of Legendary Libido: Appeared in the movie A Tale of Legendary Libido as Dan-bi, one of the many women trying to seduce the main character in the village [12][13]

2011-present

[edit]

She was cast as Mi-ra in the movie Wonderful Radio.[14]
