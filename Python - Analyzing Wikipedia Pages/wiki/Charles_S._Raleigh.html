Charles Sidney Raleigh (1831 – 1925) was a British-American painter born in Gloucester, England.

Early life and career

[edit]

Charles Sidney Raleigh was born in 1831 in Gloucester, England; he ran away when he was ten years old and spent the next thirty years as a sailor and merchant seaman.[1] After joining the merchant navy, he got married and settled in Massachusetts.[2]

Raleigh often advertised himself as a “house, sign, ornamental, and carriage painter", although he painted over six hundred paintings of vessels.[3] Raleigh's most famous painting is the Law of the Wild, painted in 1881.[4]
