Margaretha Knutsdotter Krook (15 October 1925 – 7 May 2001) was a Swedish stage and film actress. She won the Eugene O'Neill Award in 1974. In 1976, she won the Guldbagge Award for Best Actress for the film Release the Prisoners to Spring.[1] She was awarded the Illis quorum in 1995 for her performance of Gertrude Stein at the Royal Dramatic Theatre.[2][3]
