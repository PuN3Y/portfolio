From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Equipment used for work in the home

Household hardware are hardware generally used in residential settings; such as keys, locks, nuts, screws, washers, hinges, latches, handles, wire, chains, belts, plumbing supplies, electrical supplies, tools, utensils, cutlery and machine parts.[1] Household hardware is typically sold in hardware stores.[2]
