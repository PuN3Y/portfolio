Korea Midland Power Co., Ltd (KOMIPO; Korean: 한국중부발전; RR: Hanguk Joongbu Baljeon) is a South Korean power company. It has expertise in operating the process of manufacturing electrical energy and technology to build power plants.[1][better source needed] It supplies power to South Korea through wind, solar, solid waste fuels, fuel cell power, and thermal power (coal, liquefied natural gas, and heavy oil).[2]
