Billionaire is the sixth studio album by the Canadian singer-songwriter Kathleen Edwards. It was released on August 22, 2025, by Dualtone Records.[8] The album was co-produced by Jason Isbell and Gena Johnson.[9]

Background

[edit]

In a press release related to the announcement of Billionaire, Edwards stated that she named the album as such because she felt that people were using the term "billionaire" in what she described as "a caustic way", further adding: "But we should all want to be billionaires in life, to be rich in experience, friendship, purpose, and the pursuit of the things that bring us joy."[10]

Promotion

[edit]

The album's double lead singles, "Save Your Soul", and "Say Goodbye, Tell No One", were released on June 3, 2025.[11] The second single, "Pine", was released on July 3, 2025.[12] The final singles, "When the Truth Comes Out" and "Little Red Ranger", were released on July 30, 2025.[13]

Critical reception

[edit]

Writing for Pitchfork, Amanda Wicks rated the album 6.8 out of 10, and opined that Billionare "[felt] more lived in" in contrast to Edwards' previous album Total Freedom.[14] Felix Hughes of NEXT Magazine Canada wrote in his review of the album that it was a "triumphant" return to music for Edwards that is "full of confidence and brutally honest reflections on the world around us."[2]
