Karolina Szymczak (Polish: [ka.rɔˈli.na ˈʂɘ̟m.t͡ʂak]; born 25 October 1991) is an actress and model. She performed in feature films such as Hercules (2014), Stars (2017), Diablo: The Ultimate Race (2019), Politics (2019), To musi być miłość (2021), Babylon (2022), The Insurgent 1863 (2024), and Influencer Girl (2024), as well as in television series such as Oko za oko (2018), NCIS: Los Angeles (2022), and Mythic Quest (2025).

Biography

[edit]

Karolina Szymczak was born on 25 October 1991 in Złotoryja, Poland.[1] She graduated from the DSW University of Lower Silesia [pl] in Wrocław, with a degree in online advertising. Afterwards, she moved to Warsaw, where she begun working as a model. She was employed to promote numerous fashion brands in the United States and Europe.[2][3] In 2013, her photos were published in Playboy magazine.[3]

As an actress, she debuted with the role of Alcmene, in a 2014 action-adventure fantasy film Hercules, directed by Brett Ratner. Afterwards, she attended an acting school in Los Angeles, California.[2][3] Since then, Szymczak also performed in movies such as Stars (2017), Diablo: The Ultimate Race (2019), Politics (2019), To musi być miłość (2021), Babylon (2022), The Insurgent 1863 (2024), and Influencer Girl (2024), as well as in television series such as Oko za oko (2018), NCIS: Los Angeles (2022), and Mythic Quest (2025).[1][2][3]

Private life

[edit]

Szymczak began dating actor Piotr Adamczyk in 2016, and the couple married in 2019.[4] She suffers from the endometriosis.[5]
