Main article: 2020 Sibiu Open

Danilo Petrović was the defending champion[1] but chose not to defend his title.

Marc-Andrea Hüsler won the title after defeating Tomás Martín Etcheverry 7–5, 6–0 in the final.

Seeds

[edit]

  2. Quentin Halys (first round)
  3. Marc-Andrea Hüsler (champion)
  4. Francisco Cerúndolo (semifinals)
  5. Evgeny Karlovskiy (first round)
  6. Adrián Menéndez Maceiras (first round)
  7. Malek Jaziri (quarterfinals)
  8. Shuichi Sekiguchi (second round)
