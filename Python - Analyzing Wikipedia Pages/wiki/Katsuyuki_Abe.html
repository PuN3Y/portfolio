Katsuyuki Abe (阿部 勝幸, Abe Katsuyuki) is a former international table tennis player from Japan.[1]

Career

[edit]

Table tennis

[edit]

He won a bronze medal at the 1971 World Table Tennis Championships with Yujiro Imano and another with Shigeo Itoh at the 1975 World Table Tennis Championships.[2][3]
