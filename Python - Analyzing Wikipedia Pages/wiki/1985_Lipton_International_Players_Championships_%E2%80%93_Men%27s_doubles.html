Main article: 1985 Lipton International Players Championships

Paul Annacone and Christo van Rensburg won in the final 7–5, 7–5, 6–4 in the final against Sherwood Stewart and Kim Warwick.[1]

  2. Heinz Günthardt / Balázs Taróczy (second round)
  3. Stefan Edberg / Anders Järryd (quarterfinals)
  4. Ken Flach / Robert Seguso (third round)
  5. Kevin Curren / Steve Denton (second round)
  6. Broderick Dyke / John Fitzgerald (third round)
  7. Fritz Buehning / Ferdi Taygan (quarterfinals)
  8. Henri Leconte / Yannick Noah (semifinals)
  9. Joakim Nyström / Mats Wilander (quarterfinals)

  16. Peter Doohan / Michael Fancutt (third round)
