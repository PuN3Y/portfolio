Public park in Daegu, South Korea
Wolgwang Waterside Park
Hangul
월광수변공원
Hanja
月光水邊公園
RRWolgwang subyeon gongwon
MRWŏlgwang subyŏn kongwŏn

Wolgwang Water's Edge Park is a park that is located in Daegu Dalseo-gu, South Korea. The area of park is 40,163 m2. It opened in 2000 April. As there are a lot of roses around the park, a rose festival is held in spring.
