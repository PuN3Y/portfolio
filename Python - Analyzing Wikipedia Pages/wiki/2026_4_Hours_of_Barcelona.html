From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Endurance sportscar racing event

The 2026 4 Hours of Barcelona was an endurance sportscar racing event, held between 10 and 12 April 2026 at Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya in Montmeló, Spain. It was the first of six rounds of the 2026 European Le Mans Series season, and the sixth consecutive running of the event as part of the championship.

Entry list

[edit]
Main article: 2026 European Le Mans Series § Entries

The provisional entry list was published on 1 April 2026 and consisted of 47 entries across 4 categories – 11 in LMP2, 12 in LMP2 Pro-Am, 10 in LMP3, and 14 in LMGT3.[1]

Free practice

[edit]

Two practice sessions were held before the event: one on Friday, and one on Saturday. The session on Friday afternoon lasted 90 minutes, and the session on Saturday morning also lasted 90 minutes.[2]

Race

[edit]

The race started at 12:00 CEST, and ran for 4 hours.[2] The race was won by the No. 29 Forestier Racing by Panis Oreca 07, driven by Oliver Gray, Esteban Masson and Louis Rousset.[13]
