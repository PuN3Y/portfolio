The 1956 U.S. Figure Skating Championships were held from March 14-17 at the Philadelphia Arena in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.[3] Gold, silver, and bronze medals were awarded in four disciplines – men's singles, women's singles, pair skating, and ice dancing – across three levels: senior, junior, and novice.
