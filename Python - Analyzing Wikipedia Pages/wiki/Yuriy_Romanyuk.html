Yuriy Ivanovych Romanyuk (Ukrainian: Юрій Іванович Романюк; born 6 May 1997) is a Ukrainian professional footballer who plays as a midfielder.

Career

[edit]

Romanyuk is a product of the FC Volyn Youth Sportive School System. Then he signed a professional contract with FC Volyn Lutsk in the Ukrainian Premier League.[citation needed]

He made his debut in the Ukrainian Premier League for FC Volyn on 20 March 2016, playing in a match against FC Olimpik Donetsk.[1]

Chornomorets Odesa

[edit]

On 8 July 2025, Romanyuk joined Chornomorets Odesa.[2] On 2 August 2025 in the 1st round match of Ukrainian First League between FC Chornomorets and Nyva Ternopil Romanyuk made his official debut as player of Chornomorets.[3] Romanyuk left the team on June 30, 2026.[4]

On 17 July 2026, Romanyuk joined Mayak Sarny [uk].
