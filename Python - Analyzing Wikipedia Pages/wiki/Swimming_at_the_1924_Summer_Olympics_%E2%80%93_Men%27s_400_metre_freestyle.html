The men's 400 metre freestyle was a swimming event held as part of the swimming at the 1924 Summer Olympics programme.[1] It was the fourth appearance of the event, which was established in 1908. The competition was held from Wednesday July 16, 1924 to Friday July 18, 1924.

Records

[edit]

These were the standing world and Olympic records (in minutes) prior to the 1924 Summer Olympics.

World record 4:57.0 Johnny Weissmuller New Haven (USA) March 6, 1923
Olympic record 5:24.4 George Hodgson Stockholm (SWE) July 14, 1912

In the first heat Ralph Breyer set a new Olympic record with 5:22.4 minutes, only to be bettered in the third heat by Johnny Weissmuller who set a time of 5:22.2 minutes. Weissmuller improved this record to 5:13.6 minutes in the semi-finals and finally to 5:04.2 minutes in the final.
