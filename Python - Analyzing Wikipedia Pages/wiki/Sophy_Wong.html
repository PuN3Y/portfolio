Sophy Wong (simplified Chinese: 王嘉仪; traditional Chinese: 王嘉儀; pinyin: Wáng Jiā Yí; born 16 February 1992), also known as SOPHY, is a Hong Kong singer-songwriter.

Career

[edit]

Wong came 7th in The Voice (Hong Kong) in 2009. She released her debut EP, Sophrology EP [3] and formed her record label, Bunny Eats Ltd with Tsang-Hei Chiu in 2017. She followed up with her second EP, QUARTER EP [4]
