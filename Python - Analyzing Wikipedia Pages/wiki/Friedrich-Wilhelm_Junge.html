Friedrich-Wilhelm Junge (15 July 1938 – 20 February 2025) was a German actor.[1][2] He was the founder of the Theater Dresdner Brettl (Theaterkahn Dresden).[3][4][5]

Junge was born on 15 July 1938 in Schwerin, studying at the Hans Otto Theatre Academy in Leipzig from 1957 to 1960. He died on 20 February 2025, at the age of 86.[6]
