Sathi Savithri (pronunciationⓘ) is a 1965 Indian Kannada-language film, directed by P. R. Kaundanya and produced by N. Vishweshwariah. The film stars Rajkumar, Udaykumar, Narasimharaju and K. S. Ashwath. The musical score for the film was composed by G. K. Venkatesh.[1][2]
