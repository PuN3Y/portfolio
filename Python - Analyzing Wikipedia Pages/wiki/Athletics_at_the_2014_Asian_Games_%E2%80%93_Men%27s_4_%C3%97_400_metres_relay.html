Main article: Athletics at the 2014 Asian Games

The men's 4 × 400 metres relay event at the 2014 Asian Games was held at the Incheon Asiad Main Stadium, Incheon, South Korea on 29 September – 2 October.

Schedule

[edit]

All times are Korea Standard Time (UTC+09:00)

Date Time Event
Monday, 29 September 201420:35Round 1
Thursday, 2 October 201420:20Final

Records

[edit]

Prior to this competition, the existing world, Asian and Games records were as follows.

World Record United States2:54.29Stuttgart, Germany22 August 1993
Asian Record Japan3:00.76Atlanta, United States3 August 1996
Games Record Japan3:01.70Bangkok, Thailand19 December 1998
