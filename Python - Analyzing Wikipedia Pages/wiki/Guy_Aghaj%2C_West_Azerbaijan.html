Guy Aghaj (Persian: گوي اغاج)[a] is a village in Saruq Rural District[4] of Takht-e Soleyman District in Takab County, West Azerbaijan province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 1,557 in 298 households.[5] The following census in 2011 counted 1,486 people in 338 households.[6] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 1,378 people in 405 households.[2]
