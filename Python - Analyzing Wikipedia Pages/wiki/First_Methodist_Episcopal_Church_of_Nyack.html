First Methodist Episcopal Church of Nyack, also known as Old Stone Church, is a historic Methodist Episcopal church on North Broadway, south of the junction of North Broadway and Birchwood Avenue in Upper Nyack, Rockland County, New York. It was built in 1812-1813 and is a one-story, three by two bay, sandstone building on a stone foundation. It has a moderately pitched gable roof.[2]

It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1998.[1] It was purchased by the village of Upper Nyack in 2007, and has since been used as a village meeting place and made available for private events.[3]
