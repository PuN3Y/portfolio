The women's 100 metres at the 1974 European Athletics Championships was held in Rome, Italy, at Stadio Olimpico on 2 and 3 September 1974.[1]

Participation

[edit]

According to an unofficial count, 23 athletes from 12 countries participated in the event.

  •  Austria (1)
  •  East Germany (3)
  •  Finland (1)
  •  France (1)
  •  Hungary (2)
  •  Italy (3)
  •  Netherlands (1)
  •  Poland (2)
  •  Soviet Union (2)
  •  Sweden (1)
  •  Great Britain (3)
  •  West Germany (3)
