Adakanahalli is a village in the southern state of Karnataka, India.[1][2] It is located in the Nanjangud taluk of Mysore district.

2016 Scout camp

[edit]

In 2016, Adakanahalli received 30,000 visitors who came to attend a national Scouts and Guides camp.[3]
