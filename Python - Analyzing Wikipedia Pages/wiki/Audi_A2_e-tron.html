The Audi A2 e-tron is an upcoming compact electric vehicle from Audi, positioned as the brand’s new entry-level model and planned for a launch in 2027. The model will be officially presented in autumn 2026.[1][2]

It is expected to slot below the Audi Q4 e-tron in Audi’s lineup and may take up naming variants such as “Q2 e-tron”, or even resurrect the historic “A2” badge (in-line with the original Audi A2 from 1999).[3]
