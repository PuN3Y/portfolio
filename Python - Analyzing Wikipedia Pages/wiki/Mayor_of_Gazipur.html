The Mayor of Gazipur is the chief elected executive of the Gazipur City Corporation. The Mayor's office oversees civic services, manages public properties, and coordinates the functions of various government agencies within the city. In addition, the Mayor is responsible for enforcing city corporation regulations and state laws, thereby ensuring good governance and the sustainable development of Gazipur.[1]

The mayor's office is located in Nagar Bhaban; it has jurisdiction over all 57 wards of Gazipur City.[citation needed]
