Mi Plan Remixes is a remix album by Canadian singer-songwriter Nelly Furtado, released on October 25, 2010 by Nelstar Entertainment. The album consists of twelve remixes of songs from her first Spanish album Mi Plan.

Critical reception

[edit]
Professional ratings
Review scores
SourceRating
AllMusic[1]

Stephen Thomas Erlewine of AllMusic proclaimed, "while there's nothing quite essential among the remixes, they're all enjoyable and possess a more pronounced beat."[1]
