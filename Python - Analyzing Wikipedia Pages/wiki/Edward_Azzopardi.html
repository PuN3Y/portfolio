Edward Azzopardi (born 13 December 1977) is a Maltese professional footballer manager and retired player.

Coach Career

[edit]

Birżebbuġa St. Peter's F.C.

[edit]

In 2023, he was appointed as coach of Birżebbuġa St. Peter's. In February 2025, he resigned as coach and was replaced by Edgar Degabriele.[1][2]
