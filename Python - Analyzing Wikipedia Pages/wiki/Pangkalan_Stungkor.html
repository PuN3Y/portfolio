Pangkalan Stungkor (also known as Pang Stungkor) is a settlement in Lundu District, Kuching Division, Sarawak, Malaysia.[1][2] It lies approximately 47.3 kilometres (29 mi) west-south-west of the state capital Kuching.

Neighbouring settlements include:

  • Kampung Stungkor 5.2 kilometres (3.2 mi) southeast
  • Kampung Rasau Lalang 5.2 kilometres (3.2 mi) southwest
  • Kampung Munti 7.6 kilometres (4.7 mi) west
  • Kampung Selampit 9.3 kilometres (5.8 mi) northwest
  • Kampung Perian 9.3 kilometres (5.8 mi) northwest
  • Kampung Kabong 11.7 kilometres (7.3 mi) west
  • Kampung Rasa 11.9 kilometres (7.4 mi) northwest
  • Kampung Skebang 13.1 kilometres (8.1 mi) southeast
  • Kampung Sekebang Opar 13.4 kilometres (8.3 mi) southeast
  • Kampung Sebandi Ulu 13.4 kilometres (8.3 mi) northwest
