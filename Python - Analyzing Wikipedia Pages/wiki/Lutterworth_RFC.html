Lutterworth Rugby Football Club (KRFC) is a rugby union club located in Lutterworth, Leicestershire. England. The 1st XV currently competes in Regional 1 Midlands – a level 5 league in the English rugby union system – following their promotion as champions of Regional 2 East Midlands at the end of the 2024-25 season, having been runner-up in the previous two seasons.[1][2] The club is home to more than twenty teams which, in addition to the 1st XV, includes a 2nd XV, 3rd XV, a colts XV, a women's XV and junior sides.

Honours

[edit]
  • Regional 2 East Midlands champions: 2024–25
  • Midlands East 2 champions: 1995–96[3]
  • Leicestershire 1 champions: 1988–89[4]
  • Leicestershire County Cup winners: 1904–05
