Main article: 2019 Halle Open

Łukasz Kubot and Marcelo Melo were the two-time defending champions, but lost in the final to Raven Klaasen and Michael Venus, 6–4, 3–6, [4–10].[1]

Seeds

[edit]
  1. Łukasz Kubot / Marcelo Melo (final)
  2. Raven Klaasen / Michael Venus (champions)
  3. Nikola Mektić / Franko Škugor (semifinals)
  4. Kevin Krawietz / Andreas Mies (first round)
