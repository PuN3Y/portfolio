The 1981–82 Buffalo Sabres season was the Sabres' 12th season of operation for the National Hockey League (NHL) franchise that was established on May 22, 1970.[1]

Playoffs

[edit]

The Sabres lost to the Boston Bruins three games to one, in the Adams Division semi-finals.
