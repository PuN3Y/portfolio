From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Icelandic politician (1931–2011)

This is an Icelandic name. The last name is a family name, but this person is referred to by the first name Matthías.

Matthías Árnason Mathiesen (6 August 1931 in Hafnarfjörður – 9 November 2011 in Hafnarfjörður) was an Icelandic politician and former minister. He was the Minister of Finance of Iceland from 1974 to 1978.[1]
