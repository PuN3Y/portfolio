Gurchan (Persian: گورچان)[a] is a village in Khenejin Rural District[3] of Khenejin District in Farahan County, Markazi province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Language

[edit]

The inhabitants speak Tati[4]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 466 in 115 households, when it was in the Central District of Komijan County.[5] The following census in 2011 counted 392 people in 122 households,[6] by which time the rural district had been separated from the county in the establishment of Farahan County. The rural district was transferred to the new Khenejin District.[7] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 380 people in 133 households.[1]
