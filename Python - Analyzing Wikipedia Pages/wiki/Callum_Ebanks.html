Callum Ebanks (born 8 October 2002) is an English professional footballer who plays as a forward for Bedford Town on loan from National League North club Buxton.

Career

[edit]

Ebanks started playing football at the age of twelve and earned trials with Walsall and Birmingham City after joining Oldbury-based Sunday side Legion Lions.[1] He joined Cheltenham Town on a scholarship deal, and signed his first three-year professional contract with the club at the age of 17 in September 2020.[2] He played on loan at Worcester City, Tuffley Rovers, Evesham United and Bromsgrove Sporting.[3] He made his EFL League One debut for Cheltenham on 30 July 2022, in a 3–2 defeat to Peterborough United at Whaddon Road.[4]

On 20 August 2022, he joined National League club Eastleigh on loan for the first half of the 2022–23 season.[5] He was released by Cheltenham at the end of the 2022–23 season.[6]

He signed for Stratford Town in summer 2023.[7]

On 27 June 2025, Ebanks signed for National League North club Buxton,[8] making his debut on the opening day of the season against Radcliffe.[9] In December 2025, he joined Bedford Town on loan for the remainder of the season.[10]
