Novoshirokinsky (Russian: Новоширокинский) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Gazimuro-Zavodsky District, Zabaykalsky Krai, Russia. Population: 994 (2021 census);[1] 961 (2010 census);[2] 698 (2002 census);[3] There are 4 streets in this settlement.[4]
