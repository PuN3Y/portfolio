The 2015–16 Czech Extraliga season is the 23rd season of the Czech Extraliga since its creation after the breakup of Czechoslovakia and the Czechoslovak First Ice Hockey League in 1993. HC Bílí Tygři Liberec won the championship, their first ever title, after beating HC Sparta Praha in the finals by 4 games to 2.[1]
