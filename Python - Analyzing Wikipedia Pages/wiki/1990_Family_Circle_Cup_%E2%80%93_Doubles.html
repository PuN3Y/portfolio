Main article: 1990 Family Circle Cup

The defending champions were Hana Mandlíková and Martina Navratilova, but Mandliková chose not to participate. Navratilova partnered Arantxa Sánchez Vicario and successfully defended her title, defeating Mercedes Paz and Natalia Zvereva in the final, 6–2, 6–1.[1][2]

Seeds

[edit]

The top four seeds received a bye to the second round.

  1. Martina Navratilova / Arantxa Sánchez Vicario (champions)
  2. Mercedes Paz / Natalia Zvereva (final)
  3. Lise Gregory / Gretchen Rush (semifinal)
  4. Mary Lou Daniels / Wendy White (semifinal)
  5. Leila Meskhi / Katerina Maleeva (quarterfinal)
  6. Sandra Cecchini / Patricia Tarabini (second round)
  7. Regina Rajchrtová / Andrea Temesvári-Trunkos (quarterfinal)
  8. Laura Gildemeister / Claudia Kohde-Kilsch (second round)
