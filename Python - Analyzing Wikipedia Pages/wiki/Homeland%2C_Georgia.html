Homeland is a city in Charlton County, Georgia, United States. The population was 886 in 2020.

History

[edit]

Homeland was founded in 1906.[4]

Geography

[edit]

Homeland is located in southeastern Georgia at 30°51′34″N 82°1′19″W﻿ / ﻿30.85944°N 82.02194°W﻿ / 30.85944; -82.02194 (30.859445, -82.022074).[5] It is bordered by Folkston, the Charlton County seat. Homeland is the location where U.S. Routes 1 znd 23, branch off from U.S. Route 301, serving as the northern termnus of a three-route U.S. Highway concurrency that extends as far south as Callahan, Florida. Route 1 and 23 lead northwest to Waycross, while Route 301 leads north to Nahunta.

According to the United States Census Bureau, the city has a total area of 2.5 square miles (6.4 km2), all land.[6]
