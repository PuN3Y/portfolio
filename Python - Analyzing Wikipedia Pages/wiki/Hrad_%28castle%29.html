Hrad is the Czech and Slovak word for castle and is commonly used as a part of castle name, e.g. Pražský hrad (Prague Castle)[1], Spišský hrad (Spiš Castle)[2], or Bratislavský hrad (Bratislava Castle)[3]. When spelt with capital letter H, it refers to Prague Castle, and is usually used metonymically to refer to the Czech (or, historically, Czechoslovak) government.
