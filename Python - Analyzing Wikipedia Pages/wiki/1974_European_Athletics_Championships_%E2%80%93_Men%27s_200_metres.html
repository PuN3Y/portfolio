The men's 200 metres at the 1974 European Athletics Championships was held in Rome, Italy, at Stadio Olimpico on 4 and 6 September 1974.[1]

Participation

[edit]

According to an unofficial count, 28 athletes from 14 countries participated in the event.

  •  Bulgaria (1)
  •  Czechoslovakia (3)
  •  East Germany (2)
  •  Finland (1)
  •  France (3)
  •  Great Britain (3)
  •  Hungary (1)
  •  Italy (3)
  •  Netherlands (1)
  •  Poland (3)
  •  Spain (2)
  •  Sweden (1)
  •   Switzerland (1)
  •  West Germany (3)
