Spatuloricaria caquetae,[3] sometimes known as the longtail pleco,[4] is a species of freshwater ray-finned fish belonging to the family Loricariidae, the suckermouth armored catfishes, and the subfamily Loricariinae, the mailed catfishes.[2] This catfish occurs in the Orteguaza River basin in Colombia.[1] This species reaches 37 cm (15 in) in length.[5]
