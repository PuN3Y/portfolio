Agustin Cazarez (born December 4, 1989) is an American soccer player.

Career

[edit]

Cazarez played with USL PDL club Ventura County Fusion in 2014, before moving to USL Pro club Sacramento Republic on August 19, 2014.[1]

Honors

[edit]

Sacramento Republic

  • USL Cup: 2014
