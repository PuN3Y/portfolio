Goldie is an animated children's television series created by Emily Brundige for Apple TV+[3] based on her 2019 short film of the same name. Produced by Li'l Toughy and Apple Studios with animation provided by Mercury Filmworks, it was released on February 14, 2025, and has 13 episodes.[4]

Plot

[edit]

The show follows the titular character Goldie, a giant girl, and her best friends Teeny and Petey in the small town of Boysenberg.[5]

Cast and characters

[edit]

The following voice actors play these characters within the series:[1]

  • Jessica McKenna as Goldie. She was voiced by Lo Mutuc (then known as Charlyne Yi) in the 2019 short film.
  • Amari McCoy as Teeny[2]
  • Vedanten Naidoo as Petey. He was voiced by Tex Hammond in the 2019 short film.
  • Grey DeLisle as Mrs. Petunia, an occasionally grumpy elderly woman with a heart of gold and Goldie's frenemy
  • James Sie as the Mayor
  • Dee Bradley Baker as Romeo and Harold the Yeti
