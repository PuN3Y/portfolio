From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
French bridge player (born 1961)

Bénédicte Cronier (born 1961) is a French bridge player.

Bridge accomplishments

[edit]

Wins

[edit]
  • Venice Cup (2) 2005, 2011 [1]
  • North American Bridge Championships (6)
    • Freeman Mixed Board-a-Match (1) 2015
    • Machlin Women's Swiss Teams (1) 2011 [2]
    • Sternberg Women's Board-a-Match Teams (3) 2009, 2010, 2013 [3]
    • Wagar Women's Knockout Teams (1) 2011 [4]

Runners-up

[edit]
  • Venice Cup (2) 1987, 2001 [1]
  • North American Bridge Championships (3)
    • Sternberg Women's Board-a-Match Teams (1) 2012 [3]
    • Wagar Women's Knockout Teams (2) 2012, 2013 [4]
