Passavant-sur-Layon (French pronunciation: [pasavɑ̃ syʁ lɛjɔ̃] ⓘ, literally Passavant on Layon) is a commune in the Maine-et-Loire department in western France.

Geography

[edit]

The commune is traversed by the river Layon.
