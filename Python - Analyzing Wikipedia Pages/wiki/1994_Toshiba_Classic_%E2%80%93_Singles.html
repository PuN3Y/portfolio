Main article: 1994 Toshiba Classic

Steffi Graf successfully defended her title, by defeating Arantxa Sánchez Vicario 6–2, 6–1 in the final.[1][2]

Seeds

[edit]

The top eight seeds received a bye into the second round.

  1. Steffi Graf (champion)
  2. Arantxa Sánchez Vicario (final)
  3. Conchita Martínez (semifinals)
  4. Lindsay Davenport (quarterfinals, withdrew)
  5. Jana Novotná (quarterfinals)
  6. Julie Halard (quarterfinals)
  7. Sabine Appelmans (third round)
  8. Nathalie Tauziat (second round)
  9. Iva Majoli (third round)
  10. Natalia Medvedeva (third round)
  11. Ginger Helgeson (third round)
  12. Ann Grossman (third round)
  13. Marketa Kochta (first round)
  14. Gigi Fernández (second round)
  15. Marianne Werdel (third round)
  16. Lisa Raymond (third round)
