The N E Manion Cup is an Australian Turf Club Group 3 Thoroughbred quality handicap horse race for three year olds and upwards over a distance of 2400 metres, held annually at Rosehill Racecourse in Sydney, Australia in March.[1]

History

[edit]

The race is named in honour of N. E. Manion, former director of the Sydney Turf Club.[2]

Grade

[edit]
  • 1973–1978 - Principal Race
  • 1979–2005 - Group 3
  • 2006–2013 - Listed Race
  • 2014 onwards - Group 3

Distance

[edit]
  • 1973–1978 - 2000 metres
  • 1979 onwards - 2400 metres
