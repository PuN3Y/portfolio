Jan Gan (Persian: جنگان, also Romanized as Jān Gān, Jangān, and Jāngān)[1] is a village in Band-e Zarak Rural District, in the Central District of Minab County, Hormozgan Province, Iran. At the 2006 census, its population was 177, in 36 families.[2]
