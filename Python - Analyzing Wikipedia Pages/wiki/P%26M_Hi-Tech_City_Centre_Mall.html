P&M Hi-Tech City Centre Mall[3] is located in the city of Jamshedpur, Jharkhand. It has a gross lease-able (retail) area of 600,000 sq ft (56,000 m2).[4] Film director Prakash Jha and R.K. Aggarwal are the builders of the mall.[5] It is located at Outer Circle Road, Bistupur. It is the second branch of P&M mall, Patna.
