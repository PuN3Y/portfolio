Gabriel Milési
Born7 August 1947
Foix, France
Died19 March 2021(2021-03-19) (aged 73)
Venice, Italy
OccupationsJournalist
Writer

Gabriel Milési (7 August 1947 – 19 March 2021) was a French journalist and writer.[1] He was the CEO of France Inter from 1984 to 1988, Editor-in-Chief of Le Quotidien de Paris from 1988 to 1989, Editor-in Chief of Europe 1 from 1990 to 1999, and then collection director of Michel de Maule [fr]. He was a Knight of the Legion of Honour and an Officer of the Ordre national du Mérite.
