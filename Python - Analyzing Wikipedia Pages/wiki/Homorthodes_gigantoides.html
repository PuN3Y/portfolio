Homorthodes gigantoides is a species of cutworm or dart moth in the family Noctuidae first described by William Barnes and James Halliday McDunnough in 1912.[1][2][3][4] It is found in North America.[1]

The MONA or Hodges number for Homorthodes gigantoides is 10545.[5]
