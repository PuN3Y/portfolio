Swiss swimmer

Jean Jenni

Full nameJean Jenni
NationalitySwiss
DiedJanuary 1954 (1954-02)
Sport
SportSwimming

Jean Jenni (died January 1954) was a Swiss swimmer.[1] He competed in the men's 100 metre freestyle event and the water polo at the 1920 Summer Olympics.[2][3]
