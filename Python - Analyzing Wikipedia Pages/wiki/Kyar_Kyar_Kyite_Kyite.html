Kyar Kyar Kyite Kyite (Burmese: ကျားကျားကြိုက်ကြိုက်), is a 2019 Burmese comedy film starring Myint Myat, Min Maw Kun, Khin San Win, Nyi Nyi Maung, Lin Linn and Ma Htet. The film, produced by Half & Half Film Production, premiered in Myanmar on August 29, 2019.[1][2][3][4][5][6]

Cast

[edit]
  • Myint Myat as Chit Loon Thu
  • Min Maw Kun as Kyaw Gyi
  • Ma Htet as Zar Zar
  • Khin San Win
  • Nyi Nyi Maung
  • Lin Linn
