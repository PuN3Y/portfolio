Barut Aghaji (Persian: باروت اغاجي)[a] is a village in Mojezat Rural District[4] of the Central District in Zanjan County, Zanjan province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 577 in 139 households.[5] The following census in 2011 counted 556 people in 146 households.[6] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 335 people in 101 households.[2]
