The men's 400 metres hurdles competition of the athletics events at the 2023 Pan American Games was held on November 1 and 3 at the Julio Martínez National Stadium of Santiago, Chile.[1]

Records

[edit]

Prior to this competition, the existing world and Pan American Games records were as follows:

World record  Karsten Warholm (NOR) 45.94 Tokyo, Japan August 3, 2021
Pan American Games record  Omar Cisneros (CUB) 47.99 Guadalajara, Mexico October 27, 2011
