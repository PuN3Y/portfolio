Ivan Aleksandrovich Gulko (Russian: Иван Александрович Гулько; born 23 October 2003) is a Russian footballer who plays as a forward for KAMAZ Naberezhnye Chelny on loan from Orenburg.[1][2][3][4][5][6][7]

Career

[edit]

He made his debut in the Russian Second League for Orenburg-2 on 18 July 2021, in a game against Torpedo Miass.[8]

He made his debut in the Russian First League for Tyumen on 2 March 2025, in a game against Rodina Moscow.[9]

He made his debut in the Belarusian Premier League for Slavia Mozyr on 24 August 2025 in a game against Dynamo Minsk.[10]
