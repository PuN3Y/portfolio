From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Fred Frith appears on over 600 recordings. This is a selection from bands he was/is a member of, collaborations with other bands and musicians, and his solo recordings. The year indicates when the album was first released. For a comprehensive discography, see the Discography of Fred Frith by Michel Ramond, Patrice Roussel and Stephane Vuilleumier.[1]

[edit]

  • Eye to Ear (1997, CD, Tzadik, US) – collection of film and theatre music
