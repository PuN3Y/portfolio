Sivili Chuchg is a populated place situated in Pima County, Arizona, United States, very near the international border with Mexico.[2] The name comes from the O'odham sivili chuchg, which itself borrowed siwol from Spanish, meaning "onion". The O'odham phrase means "onions standing".[3] It has an estimated elevation of 2,014 feet (614 m) above sea level.[1]
