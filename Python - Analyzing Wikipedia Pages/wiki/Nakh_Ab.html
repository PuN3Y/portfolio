Nakh Ab (Persian: نخاب)[a] is a village in Miyandasht Rural District[4] of Miyandasht District in Darmian County, South Khorasan province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 124 in 32 households, when it was in the Central District.[5] The following census in 2011 counted 123 people in 34 households.[6] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 137 people in 36 households.[2]

In 2021, the rural district was separated from the district in the formation of Miyandasht District.[7]
