Anhua Qiao station[1] (simplified Chinese: 安华桥站; traditional Chinese: 安華橋站; pinyin: Ānhuá Qiáo zhàn) is a station on Line 8 and Line 12 of the Beijing Subway.[2] This station opened on December 30, 2012.[3]

Station layout

[edit]

The station has an underground island platform.

Exits

[edit]

There are 7 exits, lettered A, B, C, D, E, F and G. Exits B, D and F have accessible elevators.
