Kalinkino (Russian: Калинкино) is a rural locality (a village) in Mayskoye Rural Settlement, Vologodsky District, Vologda Oblast, Russia. The population was 36 as of 2002.[1]

Geography

[edit]

The distance to Vologda is 32 km, to Maysky is 16 km. Pochenga, Knyazhevo, Rossolovo, Tretnikovo, Zarya, Osinnik are the nearest rural localities.
