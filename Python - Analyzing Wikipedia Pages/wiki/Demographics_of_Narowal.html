This article deals with the demographics of Narowal District, Punjab, Pakistan.

As of the 2023 census, Narowal district had a population of 1,950,954 which is roughly equal to the population of Santa Clara County, California, USA and Huangpu District, Guangzhou, China. There are 281,536 households. The district has a sex ratio of 104.40 males to 100 females and a literacy rate of 75.28%: 79.89% for males and 70.49% for females.[2][3] 494,799 (25.36% of the surveyed population) are under 10 years of age.[4] 349,095 (17.89%) live in urban areas.[2] According to the 2017 census, total population of District Narowal is 1.709 million. Male population is 0.853 m (49.96%) and Female population is 0.855 m (50.04%). Archived 2024-11-30 at the Wayback Machine According to the 1998 census of Pakistan, Narowal District's population was 1,256,097, of which only 12.11% were urban.[5]

The total area of the district is approximately 2,337 square kilometres. Prior to the creation of Zafarwal Tehsil in July 2009,[6] Narowal Tehsil occupied 1,065 square kilometres while the remaining area (1,272 square kilometres) fell in Shakargarh Tehsil.

As of 2023, 97.48% of population adheres to Islam whereas 2.22% adheres to Christianity. There is also a small Hindu population in the district.[8]

Language

[edit]
Languages of Narowal district (2023)[11]
  1. Punjabi (94.3%)
  2. Urdu (3.05%)
  3. Mewati (2.28%)
  4. Others (0.38%)

At the 2023 census, 94.29% of the population spoke Punjabi, 3.05% Urdu and 2.28% Mewati as their first language.[11]
