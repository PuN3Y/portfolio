Brazilian filmmaker (born 1982)

Marcelo Caetano (born 1982) is a Brazilian film director and screenwriter,[1] whose debut feature film Body Electric (Corpo Elétrico) was released in 2017.[2]

Career

[edit]

Born and raised in Belo Horizonte, he moved to São Paulo in the early 2000s.[3] He worked in film as a casting director, an assistant director and a contributing screenwriter on various films,[4] and directed a number of short films, prior to Body Electric, which premiered in the Bright Future competition at the International Film Festival Rotterdam in 2017.[5][6]

His second feature film, Baby, premiered in the Critics Week program at the 2024 Cannes Film Festival.[7]

Personal life

[edit]

He is openly gay.[1]

Filmography

[edit]

  • Bailão - 2009
  • By Your Side (Na Sua Companhia) - 2011
  • Verona - 2013
  • Body Electric (Corpo Elétrico) - 2017
  • Baby - 2024
