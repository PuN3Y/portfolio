Miss Grand Paraguay 2021 was the 4th edition of the Miss Grand Paraguay pageant, held on July 3, 2021, at the Hotel Guaraní in Asunción.[1][2] The contest featured twenty-two candidates, either chosen through regional pageants or through central casting, competed for the title,[3] of whom the representative from Colonias Unidas, Jimena Sosa, was named the winner.[1] She then represented Paraguay in the Miss Grand International 2021 pageant in Thailand, but got a non-placement.[4]
