Warattaya Nilkuha (Thai: วรัทยา นิลคูหา, RTGS: Waratthaya Ninkhuha; born June 11, 1983) is a Thai actress, TV hostess and model.[1] She graduated with a Bachelor of Publical Sciences from Thammasat University

Personal life

[edit]

On November 16, 2018, she married actor Puttichai Kasetsin. On November 24, 2022, she welcomed a son.[2] On June 24, 2024, the couple announced they are expecting their second child.[citation needed]
