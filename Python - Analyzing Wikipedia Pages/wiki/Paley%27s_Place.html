Paley's Place was a restaurant serving Pacific Northwest cuisine in Portland, Oregon's Northwest District, in the United States.[1]

Description and history

[edit]

Housed in a Victorian home along Northwest 21st Avenue,[2] the restaurant was established by chef Vitaly and Kimberly Paley in 1995.[3][4] Thrillist has described Paley's as "an intimate bistro-style eatery".[5] Julian Smith of Frommer's rated the "upscale bistro" 2 out of 3 stars.[3]

In October 2021, owners confirmed plans to close after Thanksgiving.[6][7]

Reception

[edit]

The business was included in Portland Monthly's 2025 list of 25 restaurants "that made Portland".[8]
