Commerce Six Road is an elevated metro station on the East-West Corridor of the Blue Line of Ahmedabad Metro in Ahmedabad, India. This metro station consists of the main Navarangpura Fire Station along with Passport Seva Kendra and M.G. Science Institute in Ahmedabad. This metro station was opened to the public on 30 September 2022.[1][2][3][4]
