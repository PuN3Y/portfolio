Genus of fungi

Alveolaria
Scientific classification
Kingdom: Fungi
Division: Basidiomycota
Class: Pucciniomycetes
Order: Pucciniales
Family: Pucciniosiraceae
Genus: Alveolaria
Lagerh.

Alveolaria is a genus of fungi belonging to the family Pucciniosiraceae.[1]

The species of this genus are found in Central America.[1]

Species

[edit]

As accepted by Species Fungorum;[2]

  • Alveolaria andina Lagerh. ex Sacc. (1895)
  • Alveolaria cordiae Lagerh. ex Sacc. (1895)

Former species, A. duguetiae Viégas (1945) = Dietelia duguetiae, Pucciniosiraceae
