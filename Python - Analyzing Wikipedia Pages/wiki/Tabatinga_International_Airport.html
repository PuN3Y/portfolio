Tabatinga International Airport (IATA: TBT, ICAO: SBTT) is the airport serving Tabatinga, Brazil.

It is operated by Vinci SA.

History

[edit]

Previously operated by Infraero, on 7 April 2021 Vinci SA won a 30-year concession to operate the airport.[5]

Airlines and destinations

[edit]
AirlinesDestinations
Azul Brazilian AirlinesManaus,[6] Tefé[7]

Access

[edit]

The airport is located 1 km (1 mi) from downtown Tabatinga.

Accidents and incidents

[edit]
  • 12 June 1982: a TABA Fairchild Hiller FH-227 registration PT-LBV en route from Eirunepé to Tabatinga while on approach to Tabatinga collided with a pole in poor visibility and crashed onto a parking lot. All 40 passengers and 4 crew died.[8][9]
  • 29 October 2009: a Brazilian Air Force Cessna 208 Caravan registration FAB-2725 en route from Cruzeiro do Sul to Tabatinga made an emergency landing on a river due engine failure. Of the 11 occupants, 1 passenger and 1 crew member died.[10]
