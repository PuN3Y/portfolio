Aravelly Avanish Rao (born 2 June 2005) is an Indian cricketer who plays for Chennai Super Kings in the Indian Premier League.[1] Avanish represented the 2024 Under-19 Cricket World Cup[2] as a member of the Indian U-19 cricket team.[3][4][5]
