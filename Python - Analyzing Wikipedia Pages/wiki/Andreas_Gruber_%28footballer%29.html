Andreas Gruber (born 29 June 1995) is an Austrian professional footballer who plays as a winger for Slovak First Football League side Dunajská Streda.[1]

Career

[edit]

Gruber made his league debut in a 1–1 home draw against Austria Wien by coming on as a substitute for Daniel Beichler after 81 minutes.[2]

International career

[edit]

Gruber has been capped at youth level for Austria. [3]
