Duang Jai Akkanee (Thai: ดวงใจอัคนี; RTGS: Duang Chai Akkhani) is a 2010 Thai lakorn 1 in 4 drama series 4 Hua Jai Haeng Khun Khao (4 Hearts of the Mountain) that is aired on Channel 3 (Thailand). It starred Nadech Kugimiya and Urassaya Sperbund.[1]

Plot

[edit]

Because of the conflict between 2 families from the fathers' generation, Akkanee and Ajjima have been enemies with each other since they were kids. When they grow up and take care of their own dairy farm which there is the white fence as a border, the quarrel and fight often happens between them. However, under their harshness against each other, they secretly care about each other.

Later, when many incidents lead them to open their hearts to each other, they have to face the big barrier. Ajjima's father is still stubborn and does not accept this son-in-law-to-be. Can Akkanee get through the barrier?[2]
