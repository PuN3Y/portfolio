8th-century narrator of hadith
This article is an orphan, as no other articles link to it. Please introduce links to this page from related articles. (February 2021)

Ibn Manzar (Arabic: ابن منذر) was an 8th-century narrator of hadith.

He transmitted hadith from:

  • Ata ibn Abi Rabah[1]

His hadith were quoted by

  • ‘Abd ar-Razzaq as-San‘ani[1]
