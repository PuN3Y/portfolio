Notre-Dame-de-Lourdes (French pronunciation: [nɔtʁə dam də luʁd]) is a parish municipality in Quebec.

Demographics

[edit]

In the 2021 Census of Population conducted by Statistics Canada, Notre-Dame-de-Lourdes had a population of 787 living in 332 of its 361 total private dwellings, a change of 14.4% from its 2016 population of 688. With a land area of 81.39 km2 (31.42 sq mi), it had a population density of 9.7/km2 (25.0/sq mi) in 2021.[4]
