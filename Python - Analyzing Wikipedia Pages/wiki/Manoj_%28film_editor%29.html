Indian film editor
For other film editors, see Manoj § Film and television.

Manoj
Born
Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India
OccupationFilm Editor
Years active2006–present
AwardsKerala State Film Awards

Manoj is an Indian film editor who works primarily in Malayalam cinema.[1] In 2015, he won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Editor for Ivide.[2][3]
