Warsaw metro station

A-16 Muranów is a planned Warsaw Metro station in the Muranów neighbourhood (crossroads of Anielewicza and Andersa streets), 635 metres north of A-15 Ratusz Arsenał and 899 metres south of A-17 Dworzec Gdański.

The original plans for the construction were temporarily dropped in 1989 due to budget constraints. In September 2019, the Warsaw city council announced that the project would finally take place. A contractor was decided, having until 2026 to build the station.[1]
