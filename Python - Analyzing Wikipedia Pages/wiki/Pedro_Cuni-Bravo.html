Pedro Cuni-Bravo
Pedro Cuni-Bravo, July 2008
Born
Pedro Cuni-Bravo
OccupationArtist
Years active1980–present

Pedro Cuni-Bravo is a Spanish artist living in New York City, specializing in murals, portraits and encaustic[1] and oil[2] paintings. He teaches in Parsons School of Design[3][4] and the Cooper Union.[5]
