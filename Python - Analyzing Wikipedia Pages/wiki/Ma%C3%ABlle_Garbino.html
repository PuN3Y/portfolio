Maëlle Antonia Garbino (born 9 August 1996) is a French professional footballer who plays as an attacking midfielder or winger for Première Ligue club Les Marseillaises. She has previously played for Lyon, Saint-Étienne, Bordeaux, Paris FC, and in Italy for Juventus.

Club career

[edit]

Garbino made her league debut for Lyon against Arras on 30 November 2014.[1]

Garbino made her league debut for Saint-Étienne against Rodez on 11 September 2016.[2] She scored her first league goal against Bordeaux on 25 September 2016, scoring in the 72nd minute.[3]

Garbino joined Bordeaux in June 2018.[4] In April 2021, she won the D1 Player of the Month for February 2021.[5] Garbino scored two goals against Stade de Reims and a hattrick against Le Havre to win the award.[6] She started the 2022–23 Division 1 Féminine season strongly, scoring a brace against Le Havre.[7] The following week, she scored a goal against Dijon, and in October she scored a brace over Guingamp.[8] Garbino won the Player of the Month award for September 2022.[9] She also won the Player of the Month award for November 2022.[10] Garbino left Bourdeaux after playing more than 100 matches and scoring 22 goals in all competitions.[11]

On 8 June 2023, Garbino joined Juventus on a two-year deal until June 2025.[12] She scored on her league debut against Sampdoria on 1 October 2023, scoring in the 20th minute.[13] She left Juventus after playing 26 matches and scoring 6 goals.[14]

On 15 July 2024, Garbino was transferred to Paris FC on a two-year contract until June 2026.[15] She scored on her league debut against Guingamp on 22 September 2024, scoring in the 20th minute.[16] Garbino was nominated for the September 2024 Player of the Month award.[17]

Following the expiration of her contract at Paris FC, Garbino joined Les Marseillaises on a free transfer.[18]

International career

[edit]

Garbino has represented France at various youth levels. In November 2022, she received her first call-up to the France national team.[19]
