XV

Supercard of Honor XIV was a scheduled professional wrestling pay-per-view event produced by American promotion Ring of Honor (ROH), that would have taken place on Saturday, April 4, 2020, at the RP Funding Center in Lakeland, Florida.[1] Wrestlers from New Japan Pro-Wrestling (NJPW), and the National Wrestling Alliance (NWA) with whom ROH had partnerships, were scheduled to appear at the event.

The event would eventually be cancelled by ROH due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
