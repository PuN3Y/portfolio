Ingalgi is a village in the southern state of Karnataka, India.[1][2] It is located in the Chitapur taluk of Kalaburagi district in Karnataka.

Demographics

[edit]

As of 2001 India census, Ingalgi had a population of 5122 with 2584 males and 2538 females.[1]
