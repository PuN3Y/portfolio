From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The International Ornithological Committee (IOC) recognizes these 366 hummingbird species in family Trochilidae, and distributes them among 112 genera.[1] One extinct species known only from a 19th century specimen, Brace's emerald (Riccordia bracei), is included. This list is presented in IOC taxonomic sequence and is also sortable alphabetically by common name and binomial name.
