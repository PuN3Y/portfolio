Esther Liu (born 8 August 1988) is a Taiwanese actress, singer and television host.[2][3]

Liu began her career at the age of 14 as part of the girl group Sweety with Joanne Tseng.[4] In 2003, Liu made her acting debut in the television series Westside Story alongside Tony Sun and Wallace Huo. Since then, she has starred in several drama series including Chinese Paladin (2005), Green Forest, My Home (2005), The Concerto (2009), Falling (2013), Thirty Something (2015) and Light the Night (2021).[5]
