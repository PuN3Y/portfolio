Çeltikli (Kurdish: Mîrkulyan) is a neighbourhood in the municipality and district of Bismil, Diyarbakır Province in Turkey.[2] The village is populated by Kurds of the Beritan tribe and had a population of 1,447 in 2022.[1][3]
