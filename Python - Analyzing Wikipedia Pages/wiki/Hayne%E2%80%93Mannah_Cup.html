The Hayne–Mannah Cup was a rugby league test match played between Fiji and Lebanon on Sunday, 19 October 2014.

Background

[edit]

The fixture was in honour of Parramatta Eels players, Jarryd Hayne and Tim Mannah, who both have Fijian and Lebanese heritage respectively. Fiji won the test match by 40–28 after leading 22–12 at half-time.[1] Fiji's James Storer won the man of the match award, scoring one try in the match.[citation needed]
