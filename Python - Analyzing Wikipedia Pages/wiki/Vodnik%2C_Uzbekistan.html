Vodnik (Uzbek: Vodnik/Водник, Russian: Водник) is an urban-type settlement of Xoʻjayli District in Karakalpakstan in Uzbekistan.[1] Its population was 5,176 people in 1989,[2] and 5,900 in 2016.[3]
