Matouš the Cobbler (Czech: O ševci Matoušovi) is a 1948 Czechoslovak drama film, directed by Miroslav Cikán.[1] It stars Ladislav Pešek, Vladimír Řepa, Jaroslav Průcha, and Josef Kemr.[2]
