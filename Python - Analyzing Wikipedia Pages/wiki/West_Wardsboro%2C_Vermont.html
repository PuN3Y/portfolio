West Wardsboro is an unincorporated village in the town of Wardsboro, Windham County, Vermont, United States. The community is located along Vermont Route 100, 19.5 miles (31.4 km) northwest of Brattleboro. West Wardsboro has a post office with ZIP code 05360.[2][3]
