Jinbu-myeon (Korean: 진부면) is a myeon (township) in Pyeongchang county of Gangwon Province South Korea. The myeon is located in northern central part of the county. The total area of Bongpyeong-myeon is 331.14 square kilometers,[2] and, as of 2008, the population was 10,203 people.[1]
