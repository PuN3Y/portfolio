Garmab (Persian: گرماب)[a] is a village in Bala Rokh Rural District[4] of Jolgeh Rokh District in Torbat-e Heydarieh County, Razavi Khorasan province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 527 in 160 households.[5] The following census in 2011 counted 557 people in 160 households.[6] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 591 people in 204 households.[2]
