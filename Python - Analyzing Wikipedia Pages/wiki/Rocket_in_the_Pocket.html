Rocket in the Pocket is a 1998 electronica album released by Console. After receiving critical acclaim, the album was re-issued in 1999, 2000 and 2002.

The song "Crabcraft" – which samples Orchestral Manoeuvres in the Dark's "Sacred Heart" – was used by Björk on her 2001 album Vespertine for the song "Heirloom".[2][3]
