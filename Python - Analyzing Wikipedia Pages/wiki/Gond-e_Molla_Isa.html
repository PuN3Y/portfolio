Gond-e Molla Isa (Persian: گندملاعيسي)[a] is a village in Dasht-e Bil Rural District of the Central District in Oshnavieh County, West Azerbaijan province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 841 in 175 households.[4] The following census in 2011 counted 919 people in 225 households.[5] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 965 people in 227 households.[2]
