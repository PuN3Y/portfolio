French Polynesian football referee

Norbert Hauata (born 8 June 1979[2]) is a football referee from French Polynesia.

Hauata lives in Moorea where he works in construction.[3][4] He became a FIFA referee in 2008.[5][6] His international career includes the 2011 FIFA U-17 World Cup, the 2012 OFC Nations Cup, 2014 FIFA World Cup[7] and the 2018 FIFA World Cup.[4][8]
