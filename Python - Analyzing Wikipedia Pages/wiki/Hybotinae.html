From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Subfamily of flies

Hybotinae
Hybos culiciformis
Scientific classification
Kingdom: Animalia
Phylum: Arthropoda
Clade: Pancrustacea
Class: Insecta
Order: Diptera
Family: Hybotidae
Subfamily: Hybotinae
Meigen, 1820[1]

Hybotinae is a globally widespread subfamily of hybotid flies.[2]

Genera

[edit]
  • Acarterus Loew, 1858[3]
  • Afrohybos Smith, 1967[4]
  • Chillcottomyia Saigusa, 1986[5]
  • Euhybus Coquillett, 1895
  • Hybos Meigen, 1803[6]
  • Lactistomyia Melander, 1902[7]
  • Lamachella Melander, 1928
  • Neohybos Ale-Rocha & Carvalho, 2003[8]
  • Parahybos Kertész, 1899[9]
  • Smithybos Ale-Rocha, 2000[10]
  • Stenoproctus Loew, 1858[3]
  • Syndyas Loew, 1858[3]
  • Syneches Walker, 1852[11][12]
