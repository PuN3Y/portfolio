Dilip Lande, popularly called Mama Lande, is an Indian politician currently serving as a Member of the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly from the Chandivali constituency. He has won the elections from this constituency for two consecutive terms. He is a member of the political party, Shiv Sena (Shinde group). He is from Mumbai Maharashtra.[1][2]

Born on October 24, 1961, in Mumbai, Maharashtra, Lande has been actively involved in politics for several years. He was first elected as a corporator in the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation in 2012 and was re-elected in 2017.

Lande has a background in business and social work. He holds a degree in commerce from Yashwantrao Chavan Maharashtra Open University, which he completed in 2008.

Positions held

[edit]
  • 2012: Elected as corporator in Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation[3] from L Ward under the Maharashtra Navnirman Sena party symbol
  • 2017: Re-Elected as corporator in Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation
  • 2019: Elected to Maharashtra Legislative Assembly [1][2]
  • 2024: Re-elected to Maharashtra Legislative Assembly [4][5]
