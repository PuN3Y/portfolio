Tatsuji (written: 達治, 達次 or 辰次) is a masculine Japanese given name. Notable people with the name include:

  • Tatsuji Fuse (布施 辰治; 1880–1953), Japanese lawyer and social activist
  • Tatsuji Inouye (井上 達二; 1881–1976), Japanese ophthalmologist
  • Tatsuji Miyoshi (三好 達治; 1900–1964), Japanese poet, writer, critic and editor
  • Tatsuji Nishimura (西村 龍次; born 1968), Japanese former Nippon Professional Baseball pitcher
  • Tatsuji Nojima (野島 達司; born 1998), Japanese visual effects artist and compositor
  • Tatsuji Nomura (野村 達次; born 1922), Japanese scientist
  • Tatsuji Okawa (大川 辰次; 1904–1994), Japanese homoerotic fetish artist
  • Tatsuji Suga (菅 辰次; 1885–1945), Japanese military officer
  • Tatsuji Sugimoto (杉本 達治; born 1962), Japanese politician
