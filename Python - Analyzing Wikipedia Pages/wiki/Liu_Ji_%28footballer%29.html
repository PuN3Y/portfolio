Liu Ji (Chinese: 刘骥; pinyin: Liú Jì; born 27 December 1990) is a Chinese football player who currently plays for Guizhou Zhicheng.

Club career

[edit]

Liu was promoted to Jiangsu Sainty's first team squad in 2009. He didn't have too much chance to play for the first team and was loaned to China League One side Guizhou Zhicheng for half year in July 2011. Liu made 8 appearances in the 2011 League One season, however, Guizhou Zhicheng finished the last place of the league and relegated via League One relegation play-off which lost to Fujian Smart Hero 6-5 in the Penalty shootout.[1]

In February 2014, Liu transferred to Guizhou Zhicheng.[2]

International career

[edit]

Liu played for China U-17 in the 2006 AFC U-17 Championship.
