Wrestling at the 1994 Commonwealth Games
VenueJuan de Fuca Recreation Centre
LocationVictoria, Canada
Dates18 to 28 August 1994
← 1986
2002 →

Wrestling at the 1994 Commonwealth Games was the 14th appearance of Wrestling at the Commonwealth Games. The events were held in Victoria, Canada, from 18 to 28 August 1994. The competition featured contests in ten weight classes.[1]

The events took place in the Juan de Fuca Arena on the Juan de Fuca Recreation Centre, off 1767 Island Highway in the west of Victoria.[2]

Canada topped the wrestling medal table for the fifth successive Games by virtue of winning nine of the ten gold medals on offer.[3]
