2018 New Zealand Television Awards

Date22 November 2018

Hosted byOliver Driver

The Huawei Mate20 New Zealand Television Awards for 2018 were held on 22 November 2018 at Civic Theatre in Auckland, New Zealand. The event was hosted by Oliver Driver.[1][2]

Winners

[edit]

[3]

New Zealand On Air Best Web Series

  • Baby Mama's Club, Hanelle Harris & Mia Teirney Henry (Culture Factory)

Best Children's Programme

  • Thunderbirds Are Go, Richard Taylor, Stuart McAra, Sharon Lark, Estelle Hughes & Giles Ridge (Pukeko Pictures & ITV Studios)

Te Māngai Pāho Best Māori Programme

  • NZ Wars: The Stories of Ruapekapeka, Adrian Stevanon, Mihingarangi Forbes, Carmen J Leonard, Colin McRae & Annabelle Lee (Great Southern Film & Television)

Te Māngai Pāho Best Reo Programme

  • Waka Huia - Timoti Karetu, Ngahuia Wade, Megan Douglas & Whatanui Flavell (Scottie Productions)

Best Live Event Coverage

  • ANZAC Dawn Service 2018, Wayne Leonard & Brendon Butt (Māori Television)

Monstavision Best Sports Programme

  • No Regrets, Bevan Sanson, Brian Hitchcock, Dave Earl, Nick Quail, Clinton Grey & Howard Dunn (Sky Network Television)

Best Director: Documentary/Factual

  • Felicity Morgan-Rhind – Topp Country S3 (Diva Productions)

Screen Auckland Best Director: Drama

  • Peter Salmon – Wanted S2 (Matchbox Pictures, R&R Productions)

New Zealand On Air Best Factual Series

  • Coast New Zealand, Anna Lynch (Great Southern Film & Television)

Best Current Affairs Programme

  • Stuff Circuit, Eugene Bingham, Phil Johnson, Toby Longbottom & Paula Penfold (Stuff)

Best Pasifika Programme

  • Tales Of Time - Coconet TV, Tuki Laumea & Lisa Taouma (Tikilounge Productions)

Best Lifestyle Programme

  • Topp Country S3, Arani Cuthbert & Felicity Morgan-Rhind (Diva Productions)

Best Comedy/Comedy Entertainment Programme

  • Jono and Ben, Bronwynn Bakker (Three)

Huawei Mate20 Best Presenter: Entertainment

  • Anika Moa – Anika Moa Unleashed S1 (Rogue Productions Ltd)

Best Original Reality Series

  • The Casketeers, Anna Lynch & Susan Leonard (Great Southern Film & Television)

Stage & Screen Best Format Reality Series

  • My Kitchen Rules NZ S3, Simon Fleming (Seven Productions NZ Ltd)

New Zealand On Air Best Documentary

  • NZ Wars: The Stories of Ruapekapeka, Adrian Stevanon, Mihingarangi Forbes, Carmen J Leonard, Colin McRae & Annabelle Lee (Great Southern Film & Television)

Best News Coverage

  • One News, Tati Urale (TVNZ)

Reporter of the Year

  • Jehan Casinader – Sunday (TVNZ)

Best Presenter: News and Current Affairs

  • Mike McRoberts – Newshub (Three)

Best Actor

  • Rick Donald – 800 Words S3 (South Pacific Pictures)

Huawei Mate20 Best Actress

  • Antonia Prebble – Westside S3 (South Pacific Pictures)

New Zealand On Air Best Drama Series

  • Westside S3, Mark Beesley, Chris Bailey, Kelly Martin & James Griffin (South Pacific Pictures)

Images & Sound Best Feature Drama

  • The Dance Exponents – Why Does Love?, Carmen J Leonard (Great Southern Film & Television)

Woman's Day Television Personality of the Year

  • Hayley Holt – TVNZ Breakfast

Legend Award

  • Tini Molyneux
