Nanyang railway station (simplified Chinese: 南阳火车站; traditional Chinese: 南陽火車站; pinyin: Nányáng huǒchē zhàn is a first-class railway station in Nanyang, Henan, China on the Nanjing–Xi'an railway and Jiaozuo–Liuzhou railway operated byt China Railway Zhengzhou Group.[1][2][3]

History

[edit]

The station was built in 1970. In May 2017, work began on rebuilding the station. The existing station building was demolished and a new building constructed. The rebuilt station opened on 26 December 2018.[4] A West Square expansion is planned.[5]
