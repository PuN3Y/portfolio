Soroka (Ukrainian: Сорока) is a village in Khorostkiv urban hromada, Chortkiv Raion, Ternopil Oblast, Ukraine.[1]

History

[edit]

The first written mention is from 1564.[2]

After the liquidation of the Husiatyn Raion on 19 July 2020, the village became part of the Chortkiv Raion.[3]

Religion

[edit]
  • Church of St. Demetrius of Thessalonica (1906, built by the OCU; 1995, UGCC).[2]
