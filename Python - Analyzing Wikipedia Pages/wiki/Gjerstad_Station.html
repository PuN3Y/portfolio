Gjerstad Station (Norwegian: Gjerstad stasjon) is a railway station located in the village of Gjerstad in Gjerstad Municipality in Agder county, Norway. The station sits along the Sørlandsbanen railway line and it is served by express trains to Kristiansand and Oslo. The small station has a waiting room, parking for up to 25 cars, and bus and taxi connections to the surrounding area.[1][2]

History

[edit]

The station was opened in 1935 when the Sørlandet Line was extended from Neslandsvatn Station to Arendal Station.[3]
