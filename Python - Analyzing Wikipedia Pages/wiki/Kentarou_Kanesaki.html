Kentarou Kanesaki (兼崎 健太郎, Kanesaki Kentarō; born June 2, 1984 in Yamaguchi Prefecture) is a freelance Japanese actor, model and singer. He has performed as Genichirou Sanada from The Prince of Tennis musicals series, Tenimyu. He was employed by Ingot Entertainment until October 31, 2019. His blood type is A.
