Norawit Titicharoenrak (Thai: นรวิชญ์ ฐิติเจริญรักษ์; born 13 June 2004), nicknamed Gemini (Thai: เจมีไนน์), is a Thai actor under GMMTV and a singer under Riser Music.[1] He first gained attention through the reality television program Thailand School Star 2019 and rose to prominence for his role as Tinn in the series My School President (2022) and as Heart in Moonlight Chicken (2023).[2]

Early life and education

[edit]

Norawit was born on 13 June 2004 in Bangkok, Thailand.[3] He was nicknamed Gemini after his zodiac sign.[4] He is an only child and was conceived through in vitro fertilisation, after his parents experienced infertility.[5] His family is of Thai Chinese descent.[6]

Gemini shared that he had always wanted to be a singer.[7] He was actively involved in music in school, having been a music club leader,[8] singing and playing in three bands, namely Survival, Cyclone and Toxic,[9][10] in addition to participating in music events and contests.[11] In 2018, he attended a workshop offered by Grammy Vocal Studio,[12] under which he then performed as the lead singer of the group Septave at the Sanamluang Music Playtime Season 4 event.[13][14][15]

In May 2022, he finished his secondary education at Harrow International School.[1] He is currently pursuing an international program, Bachelor of Arts and Science in Integrated Innovation (BAScii) at Chulalongkorn University.[16]

Career

[edit]

2019–2021: Career beginnings

[edit]

At the age of fifteen, Gemini joined GMMTV's reality TV show Thailand School Star 2019, where he finished as one of the finalists and signed an exclusive contract with GMMTV. He also met Nattawat Jirochtikul (Fourth), who would become his future acting partner.[4]

2022–2023: Breakthrough with My School President and Moonlight Chicken

[edit]

Gemini earned his breakthrough when he played his first lead role as Tinn in My School President (2022), starring alongside Fourth.[17] The show takes a musical approach by incorporating songs performed by the cast into the storytelling. Gemini participated on the soundtrack, releasing his first solo single "เพลงรัก (Hook)".[18] Gemini later reprised his role as Tinn in May 2023 in two episodes of the anthology series Our Skyy 2.[19]

While My School President was still ongoing, Moonlight Chicken aired in early 2023, where Gemini starred as Heart, a young teenager who was sheltered from the world by his parents after developing a hearing impairment. Heart builds a romantic connection with Li Ming (also played by Fourth), who learns to communicate with him through sign language and helps him to step out from isolation.[4][20]

The explosive popularity of My School President and Moonlight Chicken propelled Gemini and Fourth to significant fame and the duo quickly became popular as "GeminiFourth".[21][22] Their fandom was given the name "Khunnoo" (คุณหนู), a title used to address a 'young master', heard in episode 4 of My School President.[23] The cast of My School President went on to perform live on stage and met fans across Asia.[24] Gemini and Fourth also gained recognition for their roles, receiving awards such as Popular Couple Artists at the Golden Kinnaree Awards,[25] Hottest Artist Award at the Kazz Awards,[26] Breakthrough Actors from GQ Men of the Year 2023,[27] and Thai Best Artist at the 33rd Seoul Music Awards.[28]

In March 2023, following the success of My School President, Gemini and Fourth went on to further establish themselves as artists with the release of their single "เขินให้หน่อย (You're Blushing?)", a city pop track co-produced by Now of Mirrr and songwriter Third of Tilly Birds.[29] In August, Gemini released his first solo single "เอาไรว่ามา (Anything You Want)".[30]

In June 2023, Gemini and Fourth participated in the Love Out Loud Fan Fest 2023: Lovolution concert alongside other GMMTV artists.[31] In August, they headlined their first concert together, Gemini Fourth My Turn Concert. Due to overwhelming interest in concert tickets, a second date was added.[32]

2024–2025: Riser Music, My Love Mix-Up!, Run the World Concert tour and The Dark Dice

[edit]

Gemini was revealed as a new artist under Riser Music in February 2024.[33] In March, he released his single "ใกล้เกิน (Too Close To Handle)" under the new label.[34] He collaborated with singer-songwriter Tee of Only Monday to co-write "วัน(ที่)ดี (Good Day)", which was released in July, recorded by Inn Sarin for the soundtrack of Wandee Goodday (2024).[35] Gemini went on to collaborate with rapper UrboyTJ, who produced his single "เหนื่อยหน่อยนะ (Someone Like Me)", released in August.[36] He also worked with producer and songwriter Milo of Tilly Birds for his pop ballad single "ค้าง (Still)", released in December.[37] In August 2025, Gemini and Fourth released their pop R&B duet "เรียกว่ารักได้ไหม (Is This Love?)".[38][39]

On 27 March 2024, Gemini and Fourth released their design of My Ideal Fan, named "Look Khunnoo" (ลูกคุณหนู), which would become their fandom mascot and make appearances at fan events.[40][41]

Gemini was originally set to appear alongside Fourth in the girls' love series 23.5 (2024), but in July 2023, it was announced that they had withdrawn from the series in order to prepare for their roles in a new series.[42] Gemini and Fourth went on to star in My Love Mix-Up! (2024), which premiered on 7 June 2024.[43][44] They also performed on the soundtrack for the series, where their opening theme song, "ลบยัง (Re-Move On)", won Best Theme Song at the Asian Academy Creative Awards,[45] Y Entertain Awards,[46] and Y Universe Awards.[47]

In addition to participating in the Love Out Loud Fan Fest 2024: The Love Pirates concert in May 2024,[48] Gemini and Fourth went on to headline their Run the World Concert at the Impact Arena on 31 August and 1 September 2024, showcasing their wide range of abilities, including singing and dancing, featuring guest performances by Timethai and Jeff Satur.[49] GMMTV announced that the duo would embark on the Run the World Asia Tour, continuing in Ho Chi Minh City in October and concluding on 29 June 2025 in Osaka.[50][51]

Following the conclusion of the Run the World Asia Tour, Gemini and Fourth returned to the Impact Arena for their AI-themed A.W.A.K.E Concert on 30 and 31 August 2025.[52] Gemini performed his first self-written song "What If" on the second day, with piano accompaniment by singer and pianist Tor Saksit. The song features lyrics that he previously shared on Tor's show, Piano & I, from a song he had written when he was younger.[53] Inspired to revisit the song, he worked with producer Tor and co-writer Tun Peerawicht Tunkam, who had co-produced "ลบยัง (Re-Move On)", releasing the single "What If" in November 2025.[54][55]

Gemini starred in the leading role in the supernatural drama The Dark Dice (2025), alongside Prim Chanikarn, who starred in his music video for "ใกล้เกิน (Too Close To Handle)". The series premiered on 10 September 2025.[56]

2026–present: Ticket to Heaven, The Love of Siam: The Musical

[edit]

Gemini and Fourth performed at the Riser Concert: The First Rise, held at the Impact Arena on 13–15 February 2026.[57][58] In March 2026, Gemini released his single "Dangerous", produced by singer-songwriter Hye of Paper Planes.[59] Gemini and Fourth returned to the Impact Arena to perform at the Love Out Loud Fan Fest 2026: Heart Race concert on 22–24 May 2026.[60]

Gemini and Fourth starred in the television series Ticket to Heaven, which premiered on 30 May 2026.[61] Gemini played Barth, a new student with a troubled background sent to learn at a Catholic seminary who develops feelings for the devout student Tanrak, played by Fourth.[62]

Gemini is scheduled to hold his first solo concert called Gemini Art-Venture Concert at BITEC Live on 4–6 September 2026.[63][64]

In November 2025, it was announced that Gemini and Fourth would play the lead roles of Tong and Mew in the upcoming musical adaptation of the 2007 film Love of Siam.[65] The musical is scheduled to premiere at the Muangthai Rachadalai Theatre in December 2026.[66]

Personal ventures

[edit]

In February 2023, Gemini ventured into entrepreneurship with his own clothing line named "Divine".[67]

In February 2025, he launched "Pig Me Up", his new culinary venture specializing in grilled pork jowl, also called kho mu yang (คอหมูย่าง).[68]
