Makabana Airport (IATA: KMK, ICAO: FCPA) is an airstrip serving the village of Makabana in the Niari Department, Republic of the Congo. The runway is 2 kilometres (1.2 mi) southwest of the village.

The Makabana non-directional beacon (Ident: MK) is 1.7 nautical miles (3.1 km) east-northeast of the runway.[3]
