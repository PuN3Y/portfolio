Qaid Taher (Persian: قايدطاهر)[a] is a village in Gudarzi Rural District[3] of Oshtorinan District[b] in Borujerd County, Lorestan province, Iran. It was the capital of Bardesareh Rural District[5] until its capital was transferred to the village of Jafarabad.[3]

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 1,945 in 533 households.[6] The following census in 2011 counted 1,659 people in 507 households.[7] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 1,677 people in 545 households.[1]
