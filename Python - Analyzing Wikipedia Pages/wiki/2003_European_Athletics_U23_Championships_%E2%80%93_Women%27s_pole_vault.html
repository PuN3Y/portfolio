The women's pole vault event at the 2003 European Athletics U23 Championships was held in Bydgoszcz, Poland, at Zawisza Stadion on 17 and 19 July.[1][2][3][4]

Medalists

[edit]
GoldYelena Isinbayeva
 Russia
SilverVanessa Boslak
 France
BronzeAnna Rogowska
 Poland
