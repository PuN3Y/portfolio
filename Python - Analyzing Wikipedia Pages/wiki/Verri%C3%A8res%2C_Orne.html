Verrières (French pronunciation: [vɛʁjɛʁ] ⓘ) is a commune in the Orne department in north-western France.

Geography

[edit]

A river, La Chèvre, runs through the commune.[3]

History

[edit]

In the 15th century, the parish of Verrières belonged to the barony of Villeray in Condeau. For nearly a century (1850-1950), Verrières was one of the cradles of horse breeding Percheron.[4]

[edit]

National heritage sites

[edit]
  • Eglise Saint Ouen a fifteenth century church, it was registered as a Monument historique in 1975.[5]
