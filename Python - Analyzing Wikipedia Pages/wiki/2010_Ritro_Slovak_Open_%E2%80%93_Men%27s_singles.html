Main article: 2010 Ritro Slovak Open

Michael Berrer was the defending champion but decided not to participate.
Martin Kližan won the final against Stefan Koubek 7–6(4), 6–2.

Seeds

[edit]
  1. Teymuraz Gabashvili (second round)
  2. Illya Marchenko (second round)
  3. Adrian Mannarino (first round)
  4. Michał Przysiężny (second round, retired due to a right shoulder injury)
  5. Lukáš Lacko (second round)
  6. Dustin Brown (first round)
  7. Blaž Kavčič (second round)
  8. Igor Kunitsyn (semifinals)
