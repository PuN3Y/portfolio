Opytny (Russian: Опытный) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Veydelevsky District, Belgorod Oblast, Russia. The population was 166 as of 2010.[2] There are 5 streets.

Geography

[edit]

Opytny is located 6 km southeast of Veydelevka (the district's administrative centre) by road. Viktoropol is the nearest rural locality.[3]
