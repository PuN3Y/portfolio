Henri Padou Jr. (21 August 1928 – 16 November 1999) was a French freestyle swimmer who competed at the 1948 Summer Olympics. He won a bronze medal in the 4 × 200 m relay and failed to reach the final of the 100 m race.[1]

His father Henri Padou was an Olympic swimmer and water polo player.[1]
