The Impact: Atlanta is an American reality television series that premiered on BET+ on October 5, 2022.[1][2][3]

Production

[edit]

On September 12, 2023, the series was renewed for a second season which premiered on October 5, 2023.[4]

On September 30, 2024, the series was renewed for a third season which premiered on October 17, 2024.[5]
