Main article: 2013 Aegon Championships

Marin Čilić was the defending champion,[1] but was defeated 7–5, 5–7, 3–6 in the final by Andy Murray.[2] Winning in the final meant Murray became the first British player to win three titles at the Queen's Club since Arthur Lowe in the 1920s.[3]

Seeds

[edit]

The top eight seeds receive a bye into the second round.

  1. Andy Murray (champion)
  2. Tomáš Berdych (quarterfinals)
  3. Juan Martín del Potro (quarterfinals)
  4. Jo-Wilfried Tsonga (semifinals)
  5. Marin Čilić (final)
  6. Sam Querrey (third round)
  7. Alexandr Dolgopolov (third round)
  8. Kevin Anderson (withdrew because of a shoulder injury)
  9. Benoît Paire (second round)
  10. Grigor Dimitrov (second round)
  11. Julien Benneteau (second round)
  12. Lukáš Rosol (second round)
  13. Jarkko Nieminen (second round)
  14. Denis Istomin (second round)
  15. Pablo Andújar (first round)
  16. Grega Žemlja (third round)
