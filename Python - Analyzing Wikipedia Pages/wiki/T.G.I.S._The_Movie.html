T.G.I.S. The Movie is a 1997 Philippine teen adventure film directed by Mark A. Reyes and written by Kit Villanueva-Zapata. The film stars Angelu de Leon, Bobby Andrews, Rica Peralejo, Onemig Bondoc, Raven Villanueva , Michael Flores, Ciara Sotto and Red Sternberg.[1][2][3][4][5] The movie is the film adaptation of the youth orientation series Thank God It's Sabado (T.G.I.S).

Synopsis

[edit]

Joaquin "Wacks" plans a weekend get-away with Peachy and the rest of his group of friends using his father's yacht without telling him. The boat broke down, leaving him and his friends stranded on an island.
