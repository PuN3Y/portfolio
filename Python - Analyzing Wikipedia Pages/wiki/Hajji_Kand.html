Hajji Kand (Persian: حاجي كند, also Romanized as Ḩājjī Kand; also known as Ḩājjī Kandī)[1] is a village in Akhtachi Rural District, in the Central District of Bukan County, West Azerbaijan Province, Iran. At the 2006 census, its population was 213, in 38 families.[2]
