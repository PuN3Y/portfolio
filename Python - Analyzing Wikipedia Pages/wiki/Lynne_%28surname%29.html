Lynne is a surname of English, Scottish, or Irish origin. See Lynn (surname) for derivation.

People with the surname (given or married, stage names not included) include:

  • Betty Lynne (1911–2011), British actress
  • Beverly Lynne (born 1973), American actress
  • Bjørn Lynne (born 1966), Norwegian musician
  • Carole Lynne (1918–2008), British theatre actress
  • Darci Lynne (born 2004), American ventriloquist
  • Deanna Lynne (born 1970), American television producer
  • Donna Lynne (born 1953), American politician
  • Ethel Lynne (1895–1943), American actress
  • Gillian Lynne (1926–2018), English dancer, choreographer
  • Gloria Lynne (1929–2013), American jazz singer
  • Jaime Lynne (born 1979), American wrestler
  • Jeff Lynne (born 1947), English musician
  • Judy Lynne (born 1943), American actress
  • Lisa Lynne, Celtic musician
  • Liz Lynne (born 1948), English politician
  • Michael Lynne (1941–2019), American film executive
  • Rockie Lynne (born 1964), American singer-songwriter
  • Seybourn Arthur Lynne (born 1877), Alabama politician
  • Seybourn Harris Lynne (1907–2000), American judge
  • Shelby Lynne (born 1968), American singer-songwriter
  • Walter Lynne (fl. 1550), Flemish publisher and translator
  • William Thynne Lynne (1835–1911), English author and astronomer's assistant
