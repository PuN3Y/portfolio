Other Men's Shoes is a 1920 American silent drama film directed by Edgar Lewis and starring Crauford Kent in a dual role, Irene Boyle, and Stephen Grattan.[1]

The film was translated from a Dutch print by Edward Lorusso and produced for DVD with a score by David Drazin in 2022.
