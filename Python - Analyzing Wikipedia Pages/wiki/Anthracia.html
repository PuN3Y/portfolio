Genus of moths
This article is about genus of moths. For the Arcadian nymph, see Anthracia (mythology).

Anthracia
Scientific classification
Kingdom: Animalia
Phylum: Arthropoda
Clade: Pancrustacea
Class: Insecta
Order: Lepidoptera
Superfamily: Noctuoidea
Family: Noctuidae
Subfamily: Acronictinae
Genus: Anthracia
Hübner, [1823]
Synonyms
  • Micromania Christoph, 1893
  • Scioptila Warren, 1912

Anthracia is a genus of moths of the family Noctuidae. The genus was erected by Jacob Hübner in 1823.[1][2][3]

Species

[edit]
  • Anthracia ephialtes (Hübner, [1822])
  • Anthracia eriopoda (Herrich-Schäffer, 1851)
  • Anthracia sublimbatus (Püngeler, 1900)
  • Anthracia submarginata (Bang-Haas, 1927)
  • Anthracia subsignatus (Draudt, 1950)
  • Anthracia turcomanica (Christoph, 1893)
