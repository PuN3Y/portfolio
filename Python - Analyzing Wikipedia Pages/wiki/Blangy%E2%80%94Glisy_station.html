Blangy—Glisy station (French: Gare de Blangy—Glisy) is a closed railway station in the Picardy region of France. It was served by trains of the TER Picardie regional rail network. The station is located half-way between the villages of Blangy-Tronville and Glisy. As of 2010, it is served by a TER taxi service.[1]

Services

[edit]

Two TER Picardie rail lines served Blangy-Glisy:

  • Amiens - Laon - Reims
  • Amiens - Saint-Quentin
