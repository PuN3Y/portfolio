The 35th Hundred Flowers Awards was held on September 26, 2020 in Zhengzhou, Henan, China.[1] My People, My Country received the Best Picture Award, while Frant Gwo, director of the science fiction film The Wandering Earth was crowned with the Best Director title.[2] Huang Xiaoming won Best Actor for his performance in The Bravest, and Zhou Dongyu won Best Actress for her role in Better Days.[2]
