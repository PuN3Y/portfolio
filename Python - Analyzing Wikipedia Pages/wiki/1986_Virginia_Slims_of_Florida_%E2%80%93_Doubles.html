Main article: 1986 Virginia Slims of Florida

Kathy Jordan and Elizabeth Smylie successfully defended their title, by defeating Betsy Nagelsen and Barbara Potter 7–6, 2–6, 6–2 in the final.[1]

Seeds

[edit]
  1. Kathy Jordan / Elizabeth Smylie (champions)
  2. Betsy Nagelsen / Barbara Potter (final)
  3. Bettina Bunge / Eva Pfaff (semifinals)
  4. Carling Bassett / Rosalyn Fairbank (semifinals)
  5. Jo Durie / Anne Hobbs (second round)
  6. Alycia Moulton / Catherine Suire (first round)
  7. Katerina Maleeva / Manuela Maleeva (first round)
  8. Catarina Lindqvist / JoAnne Russell (quarterfinals)
