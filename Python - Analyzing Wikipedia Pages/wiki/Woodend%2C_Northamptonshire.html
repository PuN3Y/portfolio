Woodend is a small village in West Northamptonshire, England.[1]

The village's name means 'at the end of the wood'.[2]

It is 6 miles (9.7 km) west of the town of Towcester[3] and was a hamlet in the parish of Blakesley until 1866, when it became a parish in its own right. After World War I it was designated a "thankful village", all of the soldiers it sent to war having returned safely.[4] The population of the village at the 2011 Census was 322.[5] The village is featured on Darren Hayman's album, Thankful Villages Volume 2.[6][7][8]

There is a Barrow about 250 yards east of Green's Park Farm.[9]
