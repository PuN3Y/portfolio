KRYD (104.9 FM, "97.7 & 104.9 Jack FM") is a radio station broadcasting an adult hits format in the Grand Junction, Colorado market.[2] Licensed to Norwood, Colorado, United States, the station is currently owned by Rocky III Investments d/b/a Sunshine Broadcasting, Inc.[3] KRYD is also simulcast on 97.7 KNOZ in Orchard Mesa, Colorado.
