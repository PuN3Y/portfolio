Nesa-ye Sofla (Persian: نساسفلي)[a] is a village in Pain Taleqan Rural District[5] of the Central District in Taleqan County, Alborz province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 191 in 55 households, when it was in the former Taleqan District of Savojbolagh County, Tehran province.[6] In 2008, the district was separated from the county in establishing Taleqan County, and the rural district was transferred to the new Central District.[7] In 2010, the county was separated from the province in the establishment of Alborz province.[8] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 115 in 45 households.[3]
