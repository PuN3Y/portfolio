Men's marathon 2
at the VII Paralympic Games
Paralympic Athletics
Competitors17 from 8 nations
Medalists

The Men's marathon 2 was a wheelchair marathon event in athletics at the 1984 Summer Paralympics. The race was won by Heinz Frei.[1]
