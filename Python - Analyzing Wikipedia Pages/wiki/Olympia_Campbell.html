Olympia Louise Campbell[2] (born 23 October 1995) is a British fashion model and an evolutionary anthropologist.[3] She was on the cover of Vogue Italia with her sister Edie.[4]

Early life

[edit]

Campbell was born in Westminster, to architect and British Vogue editor Sophie Hicks and hedge fund manager Roddy Campbell.[5] Her older sister is fashion model Edie Campbell.[6] Campbell attended City of London School for Girls.[7]

Career

[edit]

Modelling

[edit]

Campbell has walked for Louis Vuitton, Sonia Rykiel, Burberry, Simone Rocha, Céline, Balmain, Chanel, Fendi, Emilio Pucci, Christopher Kane, Hermès, Paco Rabanne, Ralph Lauren, and Acne Studios.[8][9]

Campbell is a feminist.[10][11] She has spoken out about sexual harassment by fashion photographers.[12]

Anthropology

[edit]

Campbell received a PhD in evolutionary anthropology from University College London in 2023.[13] She currently is a Research Fellow at the Institute of Advanced Studies in Toulouse and explores the correlates of gender-biased outcomes.[3]

In 2025, she received the Margo Wilson award for the best paper published in Evolution and Human Behavior with her article Genetic markers of cousin marriage and honour cultures.[14]
