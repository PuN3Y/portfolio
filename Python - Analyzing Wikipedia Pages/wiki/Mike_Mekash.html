American make-up artist
Mike Mekash
OccupationMake-up artist
SpouseEryn Krueger Mekash[1]

Mike Mekash is an American make-up artist. He has won six Primetime Emmy Awards and has been nominated for eighteen more in the category Outstanding Makeup.[2][3] In 2022 he won a Primetime Emmy Award for his work on the television program Stranger Things.[4]
