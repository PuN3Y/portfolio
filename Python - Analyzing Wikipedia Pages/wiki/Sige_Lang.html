"Sige Lang" is an inspirational Pinoy hip hop song by Filipino singer-songwriter Quest. Released in June 2012 as a carrier single under Warner Music Philippines, the track was arranged in collaboration with producer Bojam (Bryan Yabut) of FlipMusic.[1][2]

Background and composition

[edit]

Quest wrote "Sige Lang" with the intention of inspiring Filipinos amidst daily struggles and adversities.[1] The song features an arrangement co-produced by Bojam of FlipMusic, combining hip-hop beats with uplifting, empowering lyrics.[1][2] It was distributed digitally via iTunes and mymusicstore.ph under Warner Music Philippines.[1]

Chart performance

[edit]

Following its release, "Sige Lang" quickly gained airplay across various radio and television outlets.[2] The song peaked at No. 1 on three separate Myx music charts: Myx Daily Top 10, Pinoy Myx Countdown, and the Myx Hit Chart.[3] Metro Manila radio station Magic 89.9 also named "Sige Lang" as its "OPM Song of the Year" for 2012.[3]

Use as Gilas Pilipinas anthem

[edit]

In 2014, "Sige Lang" served as the official anthem for Gilas Pilipinas, the Philippine men's national basketball team, during their historic campaign for the 2014 FIBA Basketball World Cup in Spain.[4][5] On August 17, 2014, an official "Road to Spain" music video was released featuring highlight clips of the team set to Quest's song.[5]
