Finnish cinematographer (born 1964)

Rauno Ronkainen (born 4 August 1964) is a Finnish TV and film cinematographer and cameraman.[1]

He won the Kodak Nordic Vision Award at the 28th Gothenburg Film Festival in 2005 for his work on the acclaimed film Frozen Land.[2]
