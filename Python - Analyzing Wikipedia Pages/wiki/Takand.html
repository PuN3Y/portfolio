Takand (Persian: تاكند)[a] is a village in Qaqazan-e Gharbi Rural District[4] of the Central District in Takestan County, Qazvin province, Iran.

Demographics

[edit]

Population

[edit]

At the time of the 2006 National Census, the village's population was 370 in 115 households.[5] The following census in 2011 counted 227 people in 85 households.[6] The 2016 census measured the population of the village as 166 people in 69 households.[2]
