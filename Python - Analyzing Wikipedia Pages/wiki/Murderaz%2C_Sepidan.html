Murderaz (Persian: موردراز, also Romanized as Mūrderāz; also known as Mūrdāz-e Bozorg and Mūrderāz-e Bālā)[1] is a village in Khafri Rural District, in the Central District of Sepidan County, Fars province, Iran. At the 2006 census, its population was 32, in 9 families.[2]
