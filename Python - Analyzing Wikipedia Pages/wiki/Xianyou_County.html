Xianyou (simplified Chinese: 仙游县; traditional Chinese: 仙遊縣[1][2]; pinyin: Xiānyóu Xiàn; Puxian Min: Sing-iú gā̤ⁿ) is a county in the municipal region of Putian, in eastern Fujian province, People's Republic of China.[3]

Administration

[edit]

The county seat is in Licheng Subdistrict (鲤城街道).
