Chantrea (Khmer: ចន្រ្ទា, UNGEGN: Chântréa [cantriə]) lit. 'The Moon', is a district located in Svay Rieng Province, Cambodia. The district is subdivided into 6 communes (khum) and 49 villages (phum). According to the 1998 census of Cambodia, it had a population of 45,439.[1]
