The men's 100 metres T64 event at the 2020 Summer Paralympics in Tokyo, took place between 29 and 30 August 2021.

Records

[edit]

Prior to the competition, the existing records were as follows:[1]

World record Richard Browne (USA)10.61Doha, Qatar29 October 2015
Paralympic record Jonnie Peacock (GBR)10.81 Rio de Janeiro, Brazil8 September 2016
