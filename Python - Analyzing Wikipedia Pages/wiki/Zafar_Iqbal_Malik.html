Zafar Iqbal Malik (Urdu: ظفر اقبال ملک) is a Pakistani politician who has been a member of the Azad Kashmir Legislative Assembly since August 2021.

Political career

[edit]

Malik was elected to the Azad Kashmir Legislative Assembly from LA-8 Kotli-I as a candidate of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) in the 2021 Azad Kashmiri general election. He defeated Malik Muhmmad Nawaz Khan, a candidate of All Jammu and Kashmir Muslim Conference (AJKMC).[1]

On 26 August 2021, he was sworn into the cabinet of Prime Minister Abdul Qayyum Khan Niazi as the Minister for Higher Education.[2][3] He served in this position until the fall of Niazi's government on 14 April 2022.[4]

On 25 April 2022, he was sworn into the cabinet of Prime Minister Tanveer Ilyas Khan as the Minister for Higher Education.[5][6] He served in this position until the fall of Khan's government on 11 April 2023.[7]

On 25 June 2023, he was sworn into the cabinet of Prime Minister Chaudhry Anwarul Haq as the Minister for Higher Education.[8][9] He served in this position until the fall of Haq's government on 17 November 2025.[10]

On 26 October 2025, he joined Pakistan People's Party (PPP).[11]

On 19 November 2025, he was sworn into the cabinet of Prime Minister Faisal Mumtaz Rathore as the Minister for Higher Education.[12][13]

He was re-elected to the Assembly from LA-8 Kotli-I as a candidate of PPP in the 2026 Azad Kashmiri general election. He received 19,994 votes, while the runner-up, Malik Muhammad Nawaz Khan of the Pakistan Muslim League (N), received 12,233 votes.[14]
