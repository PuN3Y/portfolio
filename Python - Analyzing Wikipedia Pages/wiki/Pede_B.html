From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Danish rapper (born 1984)

Peter Ankjær Bigaard, better known by his stage name Pede B (born in 1984), is a Danish rapper originating from Hellerup. Starting rapping at a young age,[1] he took part in MC's Fight Night, winning the title three times in 2005, 2007 and 2009, becoming the only rapper to score a hat-trick of victories in the competition's history. He has cooperated with many rappers, and with his album Over Askeskyen very notably with DJ Noize.[2] The album reached number 3 on Hitlisten, the official Danish Albums Chart.
