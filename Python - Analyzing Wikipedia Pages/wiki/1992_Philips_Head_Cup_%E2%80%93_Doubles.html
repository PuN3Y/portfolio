Main article: 1992 Philips Head Cup

Tomás Carbonell and Francisco Roig were the defending champions, but Carbonell did not compete this year. Roig teamed up with Diego Pérez and lost in the semifinals to Horacio de la Peña and Vojtěch Flégl.

Sergio Casal and Emilio Sánchez won the title by defeating De la Peña and Flégl 6–1, 6–2 in the final.[1][2]

Seeds

[edit]

All seeds received a bye to the second round.

  1. Sergio Casal / Emilio Sánchez (champions)
  2. David Adams / Andrei Olhovskiy (quarterfinals)
  3. Ģirts Dzelde / Piet Norval (second round)
  4. Pablo Albano / Cássio Motta (quarterfinals)
  5. Luiz Mattar / Jaime Oncins (second round)
  6. Diego Pérez / Francisco Roig (semifinals)
  7. Markus Naewie / Udo Riglewski (second round)
  8. Horacio de la Peña / Vojtěch Flégl (final)
