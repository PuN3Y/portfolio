The Brenham Downtown Historic District in Brenham, Texas was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2004. Buildings in the district were designed by Alfred C. Finn, James Wetmore, and others in Classical Revival and other styles.[1] Included in the district is the Simon Theatre.

The 1939 Washington County Courthouse is also a contributing property to the district.
