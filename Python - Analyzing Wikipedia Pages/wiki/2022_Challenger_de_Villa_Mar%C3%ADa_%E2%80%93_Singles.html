Main article: 2022 Challenger de Villa María

This was the first edition of the tournament.[1][2]

Nicolás Kicker won the title after defeating Mariano Navone 7–5, 6–3 in the final.

Seeds

[edit]
  1. Juan Pablo Varillas (second round, retired)
  2. Camilo Ugo Carabelli (second round)
  3. Facundo Bagnis (semifinals)
  4. Felipe Meligeni Alves (first round)
  5. Yannick Hanfmann (quarterfinals)
  6. Santiago Rodríguez Taverna (first round)
  7. Renzo Olivo (quarterfinals)
  8. Juan Manuel Cerúndolo (quarterfinals)
