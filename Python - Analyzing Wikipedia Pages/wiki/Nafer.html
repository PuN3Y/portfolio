Nafer (Persian: نافر, also Romanized as Nāfer)[1] is a village in Donbaleh Rud-e Shomali Rural District, Dehdez District, Izeh County, Khuzestan Province, Iran. According to the 2006 census, its population was 56, residing in 9 families.[2]
