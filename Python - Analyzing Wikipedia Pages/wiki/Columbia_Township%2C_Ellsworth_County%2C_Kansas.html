Columbia Township is a township in Ellsworth County, Kansas, United States. As of the 2020 census, its population was 51.

Geography

[edit]

Columbia Township covers an area of 36.32 square miles (94.1 km2) and contains no incorporated settlements. According to the USGS, it contains two cemeteries: Fairview and Pleasant Valley.
