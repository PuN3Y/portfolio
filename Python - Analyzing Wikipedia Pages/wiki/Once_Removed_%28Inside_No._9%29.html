"Once Removed" is the third episode of series four of the British black comedy anthology television programme Inside No. 9. Written by Steve Pemberton and Reece Shearsmith, the episode was directed by Jim O'Hanlon and was first shown on 16 January 2018, on BBC Two. It stars Pemberton, Shearsmith, Monica Dolan, Nick Moran, David Calder and Emilia Fox (with the voice of Rufus Jones).[1][2][3]
