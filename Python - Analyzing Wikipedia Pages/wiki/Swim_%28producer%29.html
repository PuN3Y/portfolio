Hamish Lefevre, known professionally as Swim, is an Australian born, London based DJ and record producer.[2][3]

In November 2025, they released their second album Dear Friend which debuted at number 22 on the ARIA Charts.[2]

Career

[edit]

2020-present: In Circles & Dear Friend

[edit]

Swim's debut single "Time" was released in July 2020.

In April 2024, Swim released his debut album, In Circles.[4] The album peaked at number 64 on the ARIA Charts.[2]

In October 2025, Swim announced Dear Friend.[5] The album peaked at number 22 on the ARIA Charts.[2]
