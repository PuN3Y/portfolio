The Margaret Donoghoe Sportsground (currently known by naming rights as Aulich Park,[1] previously Allinsure Park and Dairy Farmers Park)[2] is a sports facility in Queanbeyan, New South Wales, Australia. It is situated in the town of Karabar.

History and facilities

[edit]

Since 1979, the Queanbeyan Tigers Football Club has been located at Aulich Park and supporters have raised $800,000 (in addition to grants from the Tigers Licensed Club) to install facilities such as a roofed grandstand, Interchange and Coaches Boxes for home and visiting teams, an Administration Building, ground lighting, a Hall of Fame walk, canteen, goal netting, scoreboards and disabled access.[2]
