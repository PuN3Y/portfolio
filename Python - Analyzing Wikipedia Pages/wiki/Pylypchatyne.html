Pylypchatyne (Ukrainian: Пилипчатине) is a village located in Bakhmut Raion of Donetsk Oblast, eastern Ukraine. Administratively, it is part of Soledar urban hromada, one of the hromadas of Ukraine.[1]

History

[edit]

In 2022, during the Russian invasion of Ukraine, dozens of cottages in the village were destroyed by Russian shelling.[2]
