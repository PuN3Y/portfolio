Nikol Reznikov (Hebrew: ניקול רזניקוב; born December 27, 1999) is an Israeli model and beauty pageant titleholder who was crowned Miss Israel 2018 and represented Israel at Miss Universe 2018 pageant.

Life and career

[edit]

Reznikov was born in Afula, Israel, to parents Evgeni and Victoria.[1] She studied communications in high school. Her father runs an Iscar department store and her mother is a bookkeeper. Her grandmother taught her Russian from a young age.[2][3] Her mother was born in Tashkent, Uzbek SSR, USSR.[4] Her father was born in Kyiv, Ukrainian SSR, USSR.[4][5]

In 2020, Reznikov competed as a contestant on Survivor: VIP 3, the tenth season of the Israeli edition of the Survivor franchise.[citation needed]

Pageants

[edit]

Miss Israel 2018

[edit]

Reznikov won the Miss Israel 2018 pageant, succeeding Miss Israel 2017, Adar Gandelsman.[6][7]

Miss Universe 2018

[edit]

Reznikov represented Israel at the Miss Universe 2018 pageant in Bangkok, Thailand. She was unplaced.
