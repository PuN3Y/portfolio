Bandrua is a village in the Bhopal district of Madhya Pradesh, India. It is located in the Berasia tehsil.[1]

Demographics

[edit]

According to the 2011 census of India, Bandrua has 118 households. The effective literacy rate (i.e. the literacy rate of population excluding children aged 6 and below) is 53.16%.[2]
