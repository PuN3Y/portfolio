Sukanya Srisurat (Thai: สุกัญญา ศรีสุราช; RTGS: Sukanya Sisurat; born 3 May 1995) is a Thai weightlifter competing in the 58 kg category and representing Thailand at international competitions. She competed at world championships, including at the 2015 World Weightlifting Championships.[1][2]

She was sanctioned for using the doping Methandienone.[3]

In January 2019 she was issued a four-year doping ban until January 2023 after testing positive for 5a- androstane-3a, 17 bdiol (5aAdiol) and 5b-androstane-3a, 17 b-diol (5bAdiol).[4]
