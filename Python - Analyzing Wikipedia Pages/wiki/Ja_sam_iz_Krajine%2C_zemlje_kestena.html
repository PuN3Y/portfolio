Ja sam iz Krajine, zemlje kestena (Bosnian for 'I Am from Krajina, the Land of Chestnuts') is a 2013 Bosnian film directed by Jasmin Duraković. The script for the film is based on screenwriter Mirsad Ćatić's unpublished novel Krvavi zalogaj.[1]

Cast

[edit]
  • Edhem Husić as Omer
  • Vanessa Glodjo as Dunja
  • Mirsad Tuka as Zlatan
  • Nikolina Jelisavac as Jasna
  • Emina Muftić as Ajka
    • Amra Silajdžić as young Ajka
  • Muharem Osmić as Jusuf
  • Zijah Bajrić as Agent
  • Armin Ćatić as Zlatan's father
  • Reshad Strik

Release

[edit]

The film had its premiere in Bihać on 1 July 2013.[2][3] It was shown in Sarajevo on 4 July, Tuzla 5 July, and in Bugojno on 7 July 2013.[4]
