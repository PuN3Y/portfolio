Events in the year 2017 in Azerbaijan.

Incumbents

[edit]
  • President: Ilham Aliyev
  • Vice President: Mehriban Aliyeva (starting 21 February)
  • Prime Minister: Artur Rasizade

Events

[edit]

January

[edit]
January 24 Djibouti recognized the Khojaly Massacre as an act of genocide in 2017.[1][2][3]

March

[edit]
March 14–18 First Baku International Water Week [4]
Mach 16 Started the V Global Baku Forum [5]

May

[edit]
May 5–6, 2017 4th World Forum on Intercultural Dialogue [6]
May 12–22 2017 Islamic Solidarity Games

June

[edit]
June 25 Azerbaijan Grand Prix

August

[edit]
August 1 "Sea Cup-2017" international contest was held within the International Army Games[7]

September

[edit]
September 14 Contract of the Century was signed.[8]
September 20-October 1, 2017 Women's European Volleyball Championship[9]

October

[edit]
October 30 Opening of the Baku–Tbilisi–Kars railway[10]

December

[edit]
December 21 The Parliament of Paraguay recognized the Khojaly massacre as an act of genocide in 2017 [11][12]

Deaths

[edit]
  • 19 January – Jalal Allakhverdiyev, mathematician (b. 1929).[13]
