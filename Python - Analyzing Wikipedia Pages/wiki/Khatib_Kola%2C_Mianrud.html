Khatib Kola (Persian: خطيب كلا, also Romanized as Khaţīb Kolā)[1] is a village in Mianrud Rural District, Chamestan District, Nur County, Mazandaran Province, Iran. At the 2006 census, its population was 149, in 39 families.[2]
