Tomáš Protivný (born January 24, 1986) is a Czech former professional ice hockey defenceman.

Protivný played in the Czech Extraliga with HC Sparta Praha and HC Kometa Brno.[1] He also played in the Polska Hokej Liga for JKH GKS Jastrzębie.
