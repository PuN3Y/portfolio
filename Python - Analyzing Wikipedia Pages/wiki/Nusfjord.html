Nusfjord is a fishing village in Flakstad Municipality in Lofoten in Nordland county, Norway.[2] The village lies on the southern shore of the island of Flakstadøya, along the Vestfjorden.

In 2017, Uwe Rosenberg designed and published a board game about fishing that was named after the village.[3]
