PP-9 Rawalpindi-III (پی پی-9، راولپنڈى-3) is a Constituency of Punjab Assembly.[1][2]

Area

[edit]
  • Gujjar Khan Tehsil excluding Municipal Committee Gujjar Khan and Qazian Circle Union Councils
  • Arazi Khas and UC Bishandote of Kallar Syedan Tehsil, of Rawalpindi District. Archived 2023-05-04 at the Wayback Machine

2013—2018: PP-4 (Rawalpindi-IV)

[edit]

General elections were held on 11 May 2013. Raja Shaukat Aziz Bhatti won this seat with 60,159 votes.[3]

2018—2023: PP-9 (Rawalpindi-IV)

[edit]
Further information: Pakistani general election, 2018

General elections are scheduled to be held on 25 July 2018. In 2018 Pakistani general election, Chaudhary Sajid Mehmood a candidate of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf won PP-9 Rawalpindi IV election by taking 51,686 votes.
