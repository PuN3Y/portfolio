Aubiet (French pronunciation: [objɛ]) is a commune in the Gers department in southwestern France.

Geography

[edit]

Localisation

[edit]

Roads and transports

[edit]

National road 124, which goes from Toulouse to Auch, passes by Aubiet.

Aubiet has a train station.
