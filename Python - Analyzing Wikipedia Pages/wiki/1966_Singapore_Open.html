The 1966 Singapore Open, also known as the 1966 Singapore Open Badminton Championships, took place from 8 to 10 October 1966 at the Singapore Badminton Hall in Singapore.[1][2]

Final results

[edit]
Category Winners Runners-up Score
Men's singles Yew Cheng Hoe Tjia Kian Sien 15–7, 15–1
Women's singles Nurhaena Tan Tjoen Ing 12–11, 11–4
Men's doubles Eddy Choong & Yew Cheng Hoe Khor Cheng Chye & Tan Yee Khan 15–13, 8–15, 15–2
Women's doubles Nurhaena & Tan Tjoen Ing Aishah Attan & Lim Choo Eng 15–6, 15–7
Mixed doubles Billy Ng & Sylvia Ng Eddy Choong & Lim Choo Eng 17–15, retired
