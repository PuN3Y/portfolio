Gabriela Andrada (born 1999) is a Spanish actress. She is best known for her performance in the film Ask Me What You Want.[1]

Life and career

[edit]

Born on 1999 in Madrid, Andrada is the daughter of presenter Minerva Piquero [es] and economist Juan Mario Andrada.[1][2] She earned a degree in Film and Audiovisual Arts from TAI.[3] She suffered a traffic accident in October 2021, which broke her jaw and teeth and temporarily set back the beginning of her acting career.[4]

She started her television career with appearances in series such as Sequía (2022), Los protegidos A.D.N. (2022) and Heirs to the Land (2022).[1] In the second title, she portrayed Babi, a "competitive" character adversarial to protagonist Sandra.[5] In the latter title she portrayed Dolça.[6] Early film credits included appearances in Lost & Found and Sueños y pan.[7] She landed her first leading film role in the erotic drama Ask Me What You Want (2024),[8] portraying Judith Flores, a young woman from Jerez entering a twisted relationship with her boss.[9] Also in 2024, she featured in Your Fault,[10] the second installment of the saga Culpables. In 2025, she landed the titular role in the Western adventure film Trinidad, starring alongside Paz Vega and Karla Sofía Gascón.[11][12]
