Life of Maharaja Sir Jung Bahadur of Nepal (Nepali: जङ्गबहादुरको जीवनयात्रा) is a 1909 biography of Jung Bahadur Rana, founder of the Rana dynasty, written by Padma Jung Rana.[1][2] The book gives an account of detailed information about Jung Bahadur.[3] It is generally regarded as being filial biased and lacking research.[4]
