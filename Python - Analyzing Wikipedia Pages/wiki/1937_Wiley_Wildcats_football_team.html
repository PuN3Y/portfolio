The 1937 Wiley Wildcats football team represented Wiley College as a member of the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC) during the 1937 college football season. Led by 15th-year head coach Fred T. Long, the Wildcats compiled an overall record of 5–5–1, with a conference record of 2–3–1, and finished fifth in the SWAC.[1]
