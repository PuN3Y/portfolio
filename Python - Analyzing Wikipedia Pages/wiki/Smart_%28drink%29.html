From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
Soft drink by The Coca-Cola Company

The Smart (Chinese: 醒目, romanized: Xǐngmù) soft drink is developed by The Coca-Cola Company for consumers in China since 1997.[1] It is available in a variety of flavours.[2]

Several flavours of Smart were formerly featured and available for tasting at Club Cool in Epcot at the Walt Disney World Resort in Florida.[3]
