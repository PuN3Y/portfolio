Vazman (Persian: وزمان, also Romanized as Vazmān)[1] is a village in Chehel Cheshmeh Rural District, in the Central District of Divandarreh County, Kurdistan Province, Iran. At the 2006 census, its population was 89, in 15 families.[2] The village is populated by Kurds.[3]
