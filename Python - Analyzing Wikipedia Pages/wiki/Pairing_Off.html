Pairing Off is an album by American jazz saxophonist Phil Woods' Septet recorded in 1956 and released on Prestige.[1]

Reception

[edit]
Professional ratings
Review scores
SourceRating
AllMusic[2]
The Rolling Stone Jazz Record Guide[3]
The Penguin Guide to Jazz Recordings[4]

In his review for AllMusic, Scott Yanow stated "The full group stretches out on four lengthy numbers".[2]

Track listing

[edit]

  4. "Suddenly It's Spring" (Johnny Burke, Jimmy Van Heusen) - 8:22

Personnel

[edit]
  • Phil Woods, Gene Quill - alto saxophone
  • Donald Byrd, Kenny Dorham - trumpet
  • Tommy Flanagan - piano
  • Doug Watkins - bass
  • Philly Joe Jones - drums
