From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Miroslav Cikán (11 February 1896 – 1 February 1962 in Prague) was a Czechoslovak film director.[1] He frequently worked with actors such as Hugo Haas, Jan Werich, and Vlasta Burian.
