Boreothrinax

Boreothrinax is a genus of flies in the family Pyrgotidae.[1]

Species

[edit]
  • B. debilis (Osten Sacken, 1877)[3]
  • B. dichaetus Steyskal, 1978[1]
  • B. filiola (Loew, 1876)
  • B. maculipennis (Macquart, 1846)[2]
  • B. shewelli Steyskal, 1978[1]
This list is incomplete; you can help by adding missing items. (April 2017)
