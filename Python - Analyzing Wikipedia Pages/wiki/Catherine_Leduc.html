From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Catherine Leduc is a Canadian singer-songwriter from Trois-Rivières, Quebec.[1] Formerly lead singer of the indie pop band Tricot Machine,[2] she has released several solo albums under her own name since the end of that project,[3] although her Tricot Machine bandmate Matthieu Beaumont remains involved in her solo career as a collaborator.[1]

With Tricot Machine, she won the SOCAN Songwriting Prize (French category) in 2007 as the writer of the band's single "L'Ours".[4]

Her debut solo album, Rookie, was released in 2014,[3] and she followed up in 2017 with the album Un bras de distance avec le soleil.[5]

After an eight-year hiatus, her third album, Les jours où il neige à tous les postes, was released in 2025.[6] The album was longlisted for the 2026 Polaris Music Prize.[7]
